# Collaborative Authoring - Agent Navigation Guide

## Overview

The collaboration system enables community-driven world-building through multiple phases of development, from initial creation to ongoing maintenance. The system balances creative freedom with stability, rewarding quality contributions while maintaining narrative coherence.

### Core Goals

- **Universal Creative Access**: Provide every participant with venues to create and interact
- **Narrative Stability**: Ensure core story areas remain stable for character development
- **Quality Recognition**: Reward creators who engage and delight others through reputation systems
- **Moderation Support**: Provide moderation tools without creating bottlenecks or dependency on specific individuals

### Key Principles

- **Organic Growth**: Systems naturally evolve based on community feedback and engagement
- **Trust Building**: Reputation and quality contributions increase system trust and permissions
- **Resilient Moderation**: Moderation tools work even with widespread moderator absence
- **Balanced Innovation**: Encourage new content while preserving established narrative foundations

### System Phases

The collaboration system operates through distinct phases, each with different needs and constraints:

1. **Bootstrapping**: Rapid initial world creation with minimal constraints
2. **Collaborative Storming**: Community-driven expansion and evaluation
3. **Normalization**: Refinement and connection-building in established spaces
4. **Periodic Reboot**: Special modes for revitalizing underutilized areas

### Success Metrics

- Players can find appropriate spaces for their stories without disruptive overlap
- Quality content naturally rises to prominence through community feedback
- Moderation remains effective even with limited human oversight
- System adapts to changing player patterns and preferences

## Collaboration Modes

### Bootstrapping - The Mythic Age

The bootstrapping phase occurs when the game first launches, requiring rapid creation of foundational world elements before meaningful play can begin.

#### Purpose
- Establish the basic world structure and knowledge base
- Create initial scenes and locations for player interaction
- Build a foundation that enables leverage for future development

#### Characteristics
- **Minimal Constraints**: Few system roadblocks to rapid iteration
- **Direct Canon Access**: Content can be created directly in the canonical world
- **High Moderation Energy**: Moderators are typically engaged and motivated
- **AI-Agent Integration**: Heavy use of AI generation for rapid alternatives

#### Key Activities

##### Rapid World Building
- (Potential) Generate multiple alternative world concepts for community selection
- Create high-level Knowledge items describing the world's fundamental nature
- Establish basic Rooms and Features to set narrative scenes
- Implement "Choose One" authoring workflows for quick decision-making

##### Foundation Establishment
- Define core world rules and constraints
- Create initial player-facing content and interfaces
- Establish basic narrative frameworks and story hooks
- Build essential game mechanics and systems

#### Technical Requirements
- **AI Generation Tools**: Rapid content creation with multiple alternatives
- **Selection Workflows**: Community voting and decision-making systems
- **Direct Canon Access**: Bypass normal review processes for foundational content
- **Moderation Tools**: Support for high-volume content review and approval

#### Success Indicators
- Players can begin meaningful character creation and story development
- Basic world navigation and interaction systems are functional
- Core narrative elements provide sufficient hooks for player engagement
- System has enough content to support initial player onboarding

#### Transition Criteria
- Sufficient Knowledge items exist to describe the world at a high level
- Knowledge lays out enough broad strokes for AI-generated content to be consistent
and evocative of the same themes and ambience
- Basic Rooms and Features provide adequate scene-setting
- Player feedback indicates readiness for more structured collaboration
- Moderation workload becomes sustainable for long-term operation

### Collaborative Storming

The storming phase begins once bootstrapping establishes a basic foundation, transitioning from rapid world creation to community-driven expansion and evaluation.

#### Purpose
- Enable community members to contribute personal content and stories
- Establish evaluation and feedback systems for content quality
- Create pathways for community-driven content to reach broader audiences
- Build organic systems for recognizing and rewarding quality contributions

#### Entry Conditions
- **From Bootstrapping**: Basic world knowledge and scene-setting established
- **Community Readiness**: Players demonstrate ability to create meaningful content
- **Moderation Capacity**: Systems can handle increased content review workload
- **Infrastructure**: Library and evaluation systems are functional

#### Characteristics
- **Personal Asset Creation**: Players build content in their own spaces
- **Community Evaluation**: AI and human review of proposed content
- **Temporary Access**: Event-driven content discovery and testing
- **Organic Promotion**: Quality content naturally rises through feedback systems

#### Key Activities

##### Content Creation and Submission
- Players create personal assets and story elements
- Content is submitted for evaluation and potential inclusion
- AI systems provide initial consistency and quality screening
- Human reviewers assess narrative coherence and community value

##### Community Engagement
- Events and stories draw players to discover new content
- Feedback systems collect player reactions and engagement metrics
- Community discussions shape content development and refinement
- Collaborative storytelling emerges around shared interests

##### Quality Recognition
- Feedback metrics determine content promotion pathways
- Successful content gains broader visibility and access
- Faction-based authorization systems develop for popular content
- Canon elevation becomes possible for widely-adopted elements

#### Technical Requirements
- **Personal Asset Spaces**: Individual creation areas for community members
- **Evaluation Workflows**: AI screening followed by human review
- **Feedback Collection**: Systems to measure player engagement and satisfaction
- **Event-Driven Access**: Systems to give access to Library content on a scoped temporary basis
- **Promotion Mechanisms**: Pathways for quality content to reach wider audiences
- **Faction Systems**: Group-based authorization and content management

#### Success Indicators

##### Underlying Cultural Goals
- Community has learned to collaborate effectively at scale
- Clear norms and trust have emerged around content creation
- The community has developed its own collective voice and style
- People feel confident in the evaluation and feedback systems

##### Measurable Proxies
- **Trust Indicators**: Content submission rates, feedback participation, conflict resolution success
- **Norm Formation**: Consistency in content quality, predictable collaboration patterns
- **Collective Identity**: Emergence of shared themes, faction formation, community discussions
- **System Confidence**: Usage of evaluation tools, promotion success rates, player retention

#### Transition Criteria
- **To Normalization**: Content creation rate stabilizes, crowding metrics indicate sufficient space
- **Content Quality**: Evaluation systems reliably identify high-quality contributions
- **Community Patterns**: Clear preferences and collaboration patterns emerge
- **Moderation Efficiency**: Review processes become streamlined and sustainable

### Normalizing and Maintenance

The normalizing phase emerges when the community has sufficient space and established patterns, shifting focus from expansion to refinement and connection-building.

#### Purpose
- Refine existing spaces and content rather than creating new areas
- Strengthen connections between established elements to create new play patterns
- Achieve optimal balance between space availability and player interaction
- Support sustainable, long-term community engagement

#### Entry Conditions
- **From Storming**: Community has established collaboration patterns and trust
- **From Reboot**: Reboot efforts have successfully revitalized underutilized areas
- **Content Maturity**: Existing content has proven value and community investment
- **Community Readiness**: Players are ready to focus on refinement over expansion

#### Characteristics
- **Refinement Focus**: Emphasis on improving existing spaces over creating new ones
- **Connection Building**: Creating new play patterns through linking established elements
- **Quality Maintenance**: Ongoing improvement and relevance of existing content
- **Sustainable Growth**: Controlled expansion that doesn't overwhelm established patterns

#### Key Activities

##### Content Refinement
- Improving existing spaces based on player feedback and usage patterns
- Updating content to maintain relevance and engagement
- Consolidating overlapping or redundant elements
- Enhancing accessibility and discoverability of existing content

##### Connection Development
- Creating new narrative pathways between established locations
- Building cross-faction collaboration opportunities
- Developing shared storylines that span multiple areas
- Encouraging players to explore new combinations of existing elements

##### Balance Optimization
- Achieving space sufficiency where players have enough room for their stories
- Optimizing player distribution for meaningful interaction without disruptive overlap
- Creating sustainable engagement patterns that don't require constant expansion
- Maintaining the right density of activity across different areas

##### Community Maintenance
- Supporting long-term player engagement and retention
- Managing content lifecycle and relevance
- Facilitating community events that utilize existing spaces
- Maintaining moderation and quality standards

#### Technical Requirements
- **Refinement Tools**: Systems for updating and improving existing content
- **Connection Mapping**: Tools to visualize and manage relationships between elements
- **Quality Monitoring**: Ongoing assessment of content relevance and engagement
- **Event Systems**: Support for community activities that utilize existing spaces
- **Balance Metrics**: Tools to measure space utilization and player distribution

#### Success Indicators

##### Underlying Cultural Goals
- Community values refinement and connection over constant expansion
- Players feel ownership and investment in existing spaces
- Collaborative storytelling emerges from established elements
- Long-term engagement becomes sustainable and rewarding

##### Measurable Proxies
- **Refinement Activity**: Update rates, improvement suggestions, quality metrics
- **Connection Usage**: Cross-location storytelling, faction collaboration, event participation
- **Player Investment**: Return visits, content contributions, community involvement
- **Sustainability**: Player retention, content relevance, moderation workload stability
- **Balance Achievement**: Space utilization rates, player distribution patterns, narrative conflict frequency

#### Transition Criteria
- **To Reboot**: Player base changes significantly, spaces become underutilized
- **Breakdown of Connection**: Measurable proxies decline in a way that indicates an imbalance (*probably* too much
content that the existing player base no longer engages with)

#### Stability Criteria
- **Stable Normalizing**: Community achieves and maintains optimal balance
- **Content Maturity**: Existing content provides sufficient depth for long-term engagement
- **Community Stability**: Clear patterns of sustainable collaboration emerge

### Periodic Reboot

A special collaboration mode that activates when significant changes in player patterns or community size create a mismatch between available space and actual usage.

#### Purpose
- Revitalize underutilized areas by concentrating player activity
- Adapt the world to changing community needs and preferences
- Create opportunities for new collaboration patterns and ownership structures
- Rebalance space utilization to match current player capacity

#### Entry Conditions
- **Player Base Changes**: Significant shifts in player count, preferences, or engagement patterns
- **Space Mismatch**: Available areas far exceed what the current community can meaningfully utilize
- **Engagement Decline**: Existing spaces no longer provide sufficient narrative hooks or player interaction
- **Community Readiness**: Players are open to revitalization efforts and new collaboration models

#### Characteristics
- **Concentrated Activity**: Focus on revitalizing specific areas rather than maintaining everything
- **Special Authority**: Temporary delegation of area control to specific player factions
- **Teamwork Emphasis**: Encouraging collaborative ownership and coordinated development
- **Rapid Iteration**: Quick testing and refinement of new approaches and content

#### Key Activities

##### Assessment and Planning
- Identifying which areas are most suitable for revitalization
- Understanding current player interests and collaboration preferences
- Planning targeted interventions for specific underutilized spaces
- Coordinating with interested player factions for area ownership

##### Faction Empowerment
- Delegating special authority over specific areas to player groups
- Supporting collaborative development and ownership structures
- Providing tools and resources for coordinated area revitalization
- Encouraging teamwork and shared investment in area success

##### Rapid Development
- Quick iteration on new content and approaches
- Testing different revitalization strategies in different areas
- Gathering feedback on what works and what doesn't
- Adjusting approaches based on community response

##### Community Engagement
- Creating events and activities that draw players to revitalized areas
- Building new narrative hooks and story opportunities
- Encouraging exploration and discovery of renewed spaces
- Supporting the emergence of new collaboration patterns

#### Technical Requirements
- **Area Delegation**: Systems for temporarily transferring area control to player factions
- **Faction Management**: Tools for coordinating group-based development and ownership
- **Rapid Iteration**: Support for quick content creation and testing cycles
- **Feedback Collection**: Systems to measure revitalization success and community response
- **Authority Management**: Controls for temporary permissions and area access

#### Success Indicators

##### Underlying Cultural Goals
- Players feel ownership and investment in revitalized areas
- New collaboration patterns emerge that better fit current community needs
- Community successfully adapts to changing circumstances and preferences
- Revitalized areas provide sustainable engagement without requiring constant attention

##### Measurable Proxies
- **Area Utilization**: Player activity in revitalized spaces, engagement depth
- **Faction Success**: Group collaboration effectiveness, area development progress
- **Community Response**: Player feedback, participation rates, retention in new areas
- **Sustainability**: Long-term engagement without requiring ongoing special support

#### Transition Criteria
- **To Normalizing**: Revitalized areas achieve sustainable engagement and optimal balance
- **To Storming**: New collaboration patterns emerge that enable broader community expansion

#### Transition Decision Factors
- **Choose Normalizing When**:
  - Revitalized areas provide sufficient space for current community needs
  - Players prefer refinement and connection-building over expansion
  - Community has achieved stable, sustainable engagement patterns
  - Space utilization matches current player capacity

- **Choose Storming When**:
  - Revitalization reveals new creative opportunities and community energy
  - Players demonstrate desire and capacity for broader expansion
  - New collaboration patterns suggest potential for significant growth
  - Community feedback indicates readiness for more ambitious development

## What new pieces of tech will support these collaboration needs?

Areas, where the definition of sub-areas and rooms can derive knowledge from a local store of facts and features,
would absolutely help in all modes

Factions of players are useful in all but the bootstrapping mode

A library system that is more powerful and nuanced than just a list of assets is almost a must-have for all
but the bootstrapping mode ... designing the right way to organize assets is a very difficult architectural
task, but it's going to be really central

A tool for treating _changes_ to assets as first class objects that can, in turn, be commented upon and
versioned (in _some_ ways like Git) is going to be important for drawing people together to work

Some way to reach out proactively to people _in author mode_ to notify them, for instance, that someone has
requested a review and the system "wants" them to weigh in

A tool (and probably some UI) to measure player engagement and feedback on all sorts of things will end
up being foundational to the organic self-correcting structures being planned

Likewise, a tool to report issues requiring moderation (and possibly a "soft pause" option to assure safety while
awaiting moderation) are going to be vitally important to rein in bad actors and misunderstandings

AI generation of world content that builds meaningfully upon both Knowledge and local area facts is going to
be particularly important in the bootstrapping and storming phase

AI evaluation of proposed world content for consistency and adherence to values will provide a useful first screen
in all but the bootstrapping phase
