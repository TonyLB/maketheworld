## Why this initiative exists

Three prototypes are shipped and **inert** --- the port machinery with no producer, `edgeId` (EA-8) minted by nothing, and `chainId` (P8 iteration 1) written by nothing. Each was elected on the promise that building it cheaply would buy evidence. **None of them can produce any**, because the observation each one needs is only reachable when the other two are live. P8's rollback trigger --- *a write path must decide same chain or not* --- cannot fire, since no write path can express chain membership at all.

**This vertical is the smallest configuration that makes all three observable at once.** It is not a design initiative and it settles no design question. It exists to produce one readout.

## The vertical, and why each step is load-bearing

[AB-54](dataSource/positions/AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#settled-register) is settled and supplies the mechanism:

> **Hosting kinds --- `On`, `In`, `PartOf` --- put the subordinate node in the superior's own shard** (a cup `On` a tray is a member of the tray's `ludicGraph`, not the room's). **Peer kinds --- `Under`, `Against`, `Custom` --- leave both endpoints in one graph and host nothing.**

1. **Putting the cup on the tray creates a boundary.** `On` is a hosting kind, so the cup becomes a member of the tray's shard --- **which requires the tray to be a host, an object with its own persisted `ludicGraph`.** That is [CD2h](dataSource/positions/AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#recommended-order), and it remains the substantive new mechanism however the operation is spelled. `In`/`PartOf` are deferred and cost nothing to defer --- one hosting kind is enough to make a boundary.
2. **Nesting breaks referent search, immediately.** [`roomObjectCatalogForCharacter.ts`](../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/roomObjectCatalogForCharacter.ts) enumerates the room's own `objectIds`, so the cup stops being nameable the moment it is hosted. AB-54's own rule is that **`On` admits nested things to referent-search always**, so this is a correctness fix, not an optimisation.
3. **A `Custom` relation across that boundary is the first multi-leg chain.** `Custom` is a peer kind and hosts nothing, so "tie the rope to the cup" --- rope in the room, cup in the tray's shard --- cannot be one edge. It needs a port and produces two legs: room-side and tray-side. **That is a chain, and it is the first one the system will ever have held.**

**The build order inverts 1 and 2, and the hazard above is exactly why.** [CD2h](dataSource/positions/AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#recommended-order) is gated on [CC3](dataSource/positions/AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#recommended-order) precisely because a hosted cup vanishes from flat-catalog referent search *"with no guard to fire"* --- so building hosting first ships the one state the gate forbids. Referent search goes first, built against hand-built nested fixtures: the graph already round-trips hosting shape, CD1 having found `In`/`PartOf` persistence-tested but inert. **The list above is causal order; [Recommended order](#recommended-order) is build order.**

## What already exists to build on

- **Hosting, partly --- and `On` is unauthorable today.** [Channel D](dataSource/positions/AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#recommended-order) exists to make `On` root-anchored per AB-54. **CD0/CD1/CD2 have landed, and CD2 deliberately *retired* `On` at the ingress lane** --- [`normalizeRelationSpan.ts:10`](../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/enrich/objectManipulation/normalizeRelationSpan.ts) defers `on`/`onto`/`on top of` --- so no player can say it. The real shard-hosting mechanism is **CD2h, unbuilt and gated on CC3**, which [PV1-1](#recommended-order) discharges.
- **The move operation, which is what PV1-2 extends.** [`executeObjectMove.ts`](../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/manipulation/membership/executeObjectMove.ts) is the single call site for every object membership move, **takes hosts rather than a verb** (take-hold `room -> character`, drop `character -> room`, give `character -> character`), and re-derives the carry closure and boundary sweep fresh at commit time.
- **The step sequence a compiler can emit.** [`applyObjectRelationalChange.ts`](../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/manipulation/relational/applyObjectRelationalChange.ts) already commits `[dissolveRelation*, transferMembership, establishRelation]` through `commitStepSequence` --- **as a repair path, not as the hosting model.** See PV1-2 for why that distinction decides the design.
- **Port shape, with no producer --- and the union has two branches needing two producers.** Types, terminal union and matcher shipped when the ports plan closed 2026-08-23; nothing calls `withPorts` outside tests. **`EphemeraPresencePort` gets its producer at [PV1-2](#recommended-order) and `EphemeraCrossingPort` at [PV1-3](#recommended-order)** --- the PR-15 partition, which several rules cross badly, so check which branch any port claim was instanced on before carrying it.
- **`chainId`, carried and compared, written by nothing.** The field round-trips through storage and `edgesMatch` consults it; the mutation vocabulary in [`manipulation/types.ts`](../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/manipulation/types.ts) cannot express it.
- **The commit path already mixes item kinds.** `transactWrite([multiKeyItem, ...adjacencyItems])`, so sibling records in one transaction is an established shape.

## The readout

**Written before the code, because this is the one obligation that has failed twice** --- P8's rollback trigger cannot fire, and EA-8's pinning test was declared a forcing function and did not fire when the decision came. **PV-1's entire value is that it can produce an observation, so the observation is specified first and is [PV1-0](#recommended-order).**

Provisional shape, to be sharpened by PV1-0 and not treated as settled by it:

- **Chains work** if, after `put cup on tray` and `tie rope to cup`, the two legs carry the same `chainId`, the rope is still nameable, and the cup is still nameable.
- **Chain identity is the wrong model** if the two legs cannot be given one id without a fact no caller possesses, or if the id has to be invented at read time rather than carried.
- **The explicit presence binding earns its place** if the port answers something [`EphemeraPositionAdjacency`](../../../packages/mtw-interfaces/ts/ephemeraPositionAdjacency.ts) cannot, or if the two can be made to disagree. **It does not** if nothing in the vertical can separate them --- which is itself the evidence [AB-55](dataSource/positions/AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only) wants (*does the presence port subsume adjacency?*), and grounds for taking the port back out cheaply.

Pending work is `- [ ]` and completed work is `- [X]`; mark **nested** lines `[X]` as each is done, so partial progress on a slice is visible. Updating these checkboxes is part of *done* for the slice, after tests pass.

- [ ] **PV1-1. Referent search survives nesting --- naive recursion, deliberately. First, because the gate says so.** Extend the catalog to walk into hosted objects' graphs by Dynamo read recursion. **No `ludicCache`.**
  - [ ] **Why naive is the right call and not a shortcut:** the design corpus records the cache serving referent search as *"a hard prerequisite for the constructor ever nesting"* ([CC3](dataSource/positions/AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#recommended-order)). A correct recursive read discharges that prerequisite, which **takes the whole `ludicCache` ladder off this plan's critical path** and off P8's. Replacing it with the cache later is a substitution behind a stable interface.
  - [ ] **Depth cap of five, plus a visited set.** Five is a **testing bound** --- obviously enough for this vertical, and deliberately not a claim about real nesting depth. The visited set is what makes a cyclic graph terminate rather than a depth cap doing that job by accident.
  - [ ] **Built against hand-built nested fixtures**, since nothing nests yet. The graph already round-trips hosting shape (CD1: `In`/`PartOf` persistence-tested but inert), so the fixtures are real payloads rather than mocks.
  - [ ] **Done when** a cup nested in a tray fixture can be named and acted on from the room, with a test that fails against the pre-nesting enumeration.
- [ ] **PV1-2. `On` nests, end to end --- as a *rehost carrying a containment argument*, not as a relation with a side effect.** Decided 2026-08-31 from conversation. **Moving is the primitive; containment is an argument the planner sets and the compiler lowers into kernel primitives.** Establishing an `On` edge moves nothing, and there is no operation that "establishes `On`" at all.
  - **Why this way round, recorded because the plan first said the opposite.** *"`On` re-hosts on establish"* would put the real hosting mechanism on a **repair** path: [`applyObjectRelationalChange.ts:63`](../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/manipulation/relational/applyObjectRelationalChange.ts) transfers only when `transferFromHostId` is set, which is BD-16's repair signal for a subject Plan-stage found on the wrong host. Meanwhile [`executeObjectMove.ts:62-76`](../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/manipulation/membership/executeObjectMove.ts) is already the single call site for every membership move and already takes *hosts, not an actor or a verb*. **`On` is a fourth host pair, which is the generalization that file already argues for.**
  - [ ] **The argument.** `ExecuteObjectMoveArgs`, and the plan-stage shape that feeds it, gain `containment?: 'On' | 'In' | 'PartOf'` --- **hosting kinds only**, since peer kinds host nothing (AB-54). **It is optional only because take-hold's containment kind is unnamed, not because take-hold lacks one** --- see the presence bullet below.
  - [ ] **The lowering, which adds no step kind.** `buildObjectMoveOp`/`compilePositionKernelOp` emit `[...boundaryDissolves, transferMembership, ...(containment ? [establishRelation(moved -> destination root, containment)] : [])]`. **Do not widen `MutationKernelTransferStep`** --- [`kernelStep.ts:28-33`](../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/manipulation/kernel/kernelStep.ts) treats that narrowness as load-bearing --- so the containment edge commits by the same path as every other edge. Root-anchored member -> root (LD-16), interior to the destination's shard (AB-54). **`fromHostId` stays singular**, matching today's signature; `MutationKernelTransferStep.fromHostIds` is already a set and there are known future reasons for the plural, so **do not design against the singular** --- widening it later is a signature change, not a re-architecture.
  - [ ] **Mint one presence port in the moved thing, for the host it lands in --- and no `Present` edges.** `{ portId, fromHostId: <destination>, kind: 'Present' }` on the cup's own graph ([`EphemeraPresencePort`](../../../packages/mtw-interfaces/ts/ephemeraMeta.ts)), dropped again when it leaves. **The edges are deliberately absent:** [PR-10](dataSource/positions/AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register) settled that a cover with one bucket is implicit --- *"the single bucket **is** the node list."* **What PR-10 makes implicit is the cover's edge set, not the port record**; its [derivation](dataSource/positions/AGENT.presence.discussion.planning.md#pr-10-may-presence-be-left-implicit-where-a-thing-has-at-most-one-presence-binding) says so outright --- *"this row collapses an edge set; it never says an object stops having a port"* --- and the at-most-one test is a count over presence ports, which must exist to be counted.
  - [ ] **The port rides on *any* rehost, not on `containment` being set.** Settled 2026-08-31: **take-hold is containment too** --- which kind is future work, not an absence of one --- so a cup in your hand gets a port exactly as a cup on a tray does, and the two rules never diverge. This **buys evidence for [PR-3](dataSource/positions/AGENT.presence.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only)** (*what carries presence?*), which is open; **it must not be written as answering it.**
  - [ ] **What is deferred is the multi-*ported* case, not the multi-*hosted* one.** [PR-C1](dataSource/positions/AGENT.presence.corpus.planning.md#pr-c1-the-contraption-in-two-rooms-and-whether-covers-nest) retired that predicate: `GrapplingHookGun` is **single-hosted and needs two buckets**, because **port arity is inherited from the parent.** The deferral holds exactly while nothing above the cup is itself split --- and the moment one is, the cover's edges stop being implicit *for the cup*, without the cup doing anything.
  - [ ] **Consequence: the moved thing needs its own `ludicGraph`.** Ports live on the graph record, so a port "in the cup" means `Meta::Object.ludicGraph` on the cup --- today optional and storage-only (MK2). Concepts clause 3 / LP4i mean minting a graph means minting its **root node** too, or `isEphemeraLudicGraphFieldPayload` rejects the row at the read boundary. **PV1-2 therefore mints graphs on both sides of the move:** the tray's, to hold the cup as a member, and the cup's, to hold the port.
  - [ ] **Removing the old containment edge is explicit work, and must not go through the boundary sweep.** Settled 2026-08-31: **an edge to its own graph's root node is not a boundary edge** --- it never leaves the graph, and travels with the graph wherever the graph is moved. **The code already encodes that, as a throw:** [`interactionUnderTransfer.ts`](../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/ludicGraph/expandValidate/interactionUnderTransfer.ts)'s `classifyInteractionUnderTransfer` raises on `'On' | 'In' | 'PartOf'` --- *"has no producer on an exterior graph in iteration 1 (AB-53/AB-54)"* --- so a containment edge reaching `boundaryEdgeOutcomes` **throws the transfer** rather than dissolving it. Un-hosting must drop that edge itself, sequenced outside the sweep. **And PV1-2 is precisely the event that throw's own comment names as retiring it:** *"if multi-level graphs ever land, a containment edge can appear here and this becomes a real decision again (LD-11)."*
  - [ ] **Client: `put cup on tray` routes to the *move* lane carrying `containment: 'On'`.** `On` does **not** return to the relational ingress --- `normalizeRelationSpan.ts`'s `nestingDefer` is permanently correct under this shape, and what changes is that the deferral gains a destination. **[CD4](dataSource/positions/AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#recommended-order) shrinks accordingly**: its job is no longer deciding whether or how to re-enable `On` authoring. **The reframe leaves one parse question here, not a kernel one:** *take the cup off the tray* is a rehost to a caller-named destination with no containment kind, so what does *off* resolve its destination to? Answer it in the client path.
  - [ ] **[CD6](dataSource/positions/AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#recommended-order) does *not* travel here, despite its trigger appearing to fire.** It travels to *"whichever step first writes a port record"* --- port-general wording --- but its content is **kind-agreement against an exterior edge**, which [PR-15](dataSource/positions/AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register) makes a **category error** for a presence port: it has no exterior edge to disagree with, and the shipped heal path is crossing-port-only by explicit test. **CD6 stays with [PV1-3](#recommended-order)**, and its wording is corrected at PV1-5.
  - [ ] **Done when** a cup put on a tray is a member of the tray's `ludicGraph` and not the room's, its containment edge is root-anchored inside the tray's graph, its presence port names the tray, and both are gone when it leaves --- asserted in test and observed in the running client.
