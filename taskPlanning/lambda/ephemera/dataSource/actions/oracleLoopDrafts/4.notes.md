## The change

Draft 3's two lines are an **extractive** step: they copy what `Picture` already said (the user's reading, 2026-11-09). Its form gave concrete values, "[x]: lifted and tilted" or "[y]: stays where it is", and Micro copied those in place of `Picture`. Draft 4 gives the form with placeholders only, pointing back to the source: "[x]: what Picture says [x] does". Nothing else in draft 3 changes.

The hypothesis (mine, from the user's question): in an extractive step, an example's concrete values compete with the source, and Micro takes the nearer one.
