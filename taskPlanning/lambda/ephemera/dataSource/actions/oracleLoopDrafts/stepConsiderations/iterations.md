# Step considerations: iterations

The drafts of the `stepConsiderations` read piece, for [the non-rule cell plan](../../AGENT.nonRuleCell.planning.md#recommended-order)'s step 3. The design is the [considerations proposal](../../AGENT.nonRuleCell.planning.md#the-considerations-proposal-what-to-consider-generated-on-its-own). Problems and Library entries are numbered `SC-Pn` and `SC-Ln`. The cells and inputs are the non-rule cell's, so problems shared with it keep their `NR-` ids.
