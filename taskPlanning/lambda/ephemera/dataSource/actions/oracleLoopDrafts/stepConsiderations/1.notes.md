# Step considerations draft 1: notes and reading rubric

For [the non-rule cell plan](../../AGENT.nonRuleCell.planning.md#recommended-order)'s step 3. The design is the plan's [considerations proposal](../../AGENT.nonRuleCell.planning.md#the-considerations-proposal-what-to-consider-generated-on-its-own), accepted by the user 2026-11-13, with free text (the user's call).

## Why

The non-rule cell's drafts 4 and 5 put reasoning before the finding in the same call, and the finding's categories leaked in: draft 4 hunted for an "against", and draft 5 filled four tag headings (NR-P9). The user's register is "what should I be considering?". This piece generates only that, with no finding, to see whether the set looks right. Judging the set is a later pass.

## The draft

From the non-rule cell's [`3.txt`](../nonRuleCell/3.txt):
- **Kept word for word:** Reading the scene, and Reading the steps (the securing sentence included).
- **Role:** "You judge whether…" becomes "You prepare the ground for judging whether…" (mine), since this piece gives no judgment.
- **Task, replaced:** list, per step, what someone judging it should be considering: "the things it touches, what they are like, where they are, and what the steps before it have left". Items are a few words each, by handle. The prose comes first, then a single JSON block: `{"steps": [{"step": …, "considering": [ … ]}]}`.

## Rubric (written before the batch)

- **Per cell, against its `lookFor`** (in [`stepConsiderations.ts`](../../../../../../../lambda/ephemera/calibration/oraclePieces/stepConsiderations.ts)): is each expected item present? In brief:
  - the size on OC-11, OC-18 and OC-3;
  - what is on the table, and that it comes up with it, on OC-1;
  - the cup on the saucer within reach on OC-2;
  - the chain on OC-20;
  - the tea, the glue and the cup's place on OC-6;
  - the loose saucer and cup when the table tips, then the cup's place for the pour, on h5;
  - the slide line, then the cup on the floor, on h7;
  - the cup carried on the saucer, then held within reach, on h10;
  - the trestle's weight against one person on OC-9.
- **Missing:** the decisive item absent. Above all, the size on the size cells, and on h5, what the tip does to the saucer and cup.
- **Invented items:** the player's ability, strength or technique (everywhere but OC-9); unstated weights, materials or the glue's nature. Count per cell.
- **Verdicts leaking in:** items that judge ("impossible", "difficult", "prevents", "can't"), and prose that reaches a finding anyway.
- **Headings (NR-P9):** do "things it touches", "what they are like", "where they are" and "what the steps before it have left" become sections, each filled?
- **The securing:** how the glue and the chain are written, whether as a fact ("[teapot1] glued to [table1]") or as an effect ("holds [table1] in place", "comes up with [table1]").
- **The walk:** do the later steps' lists use what the earlier steps left (h5, h7 and h10's pours)?
- **Length and form:** items per step; check failures; cut-off replies; reply lengths against the non-rule cell's batch 5 (1860 to 4178 chars).

**Against the rubric:**
- **Per cell:** the size is absent on all three size cells. What is on the table is absent on OC-1. The chain is present on OC-20. h7's slide is present and well read. h5's knock-on isn't named. h10's carried cup is missing. OC-9's weight is present.
- **The JSON is a handle list in 11 of 11:** "the things it touches" plus "naming things by their handles" became the list of handles. Everything that might bear is in the prose, if anywhere.
- **Invented items:** the player's ability in none but OC-9, where it is fair. The task's aim at things, not the player, held. But "glued to the floor" or "glued to the Parlour" is invented in OC-11 and OC-1, as in earlier batches.
- **Verdicts leaking in:** 5 of 11 reach a finding anyway (OC-1, OC-20, h5, h7, and OC-6's "fixed in place"). They come in the prose or in a closing paragraph after the block.
- **Headings (NR-P9):** the four kinds become headings in OC-11 and OC-1, and per-thing sublists elsewhere. Partial.
- **The securing:** written as an effect every time it appears: "fixed in place", "cannot be moved independently", and on h5 "would fall off". It is never written as moving with the table.
- **The walk:** h7 carries the line forward correctly. h10 doesn't carry the pick-up forward.

**What it shows:**
- **An inventory, not considerations** (mine). The task's four kinds (touches, like, where, left) ask for an inventory, and that's what came back. "Considering" was read as "involved in". What bears on the step is relational (the table's size against the cup, what the tip does to the loose things), and the inventory never reaches it. On the size cells it never reached the size.
- **The handle-only JSON is a form problem of my wording** ("a few words, naming things by their handles").
- **Without a finding asked for, verdicts still come,** in about half the replies. The register leaks less than draft 5's tags did, but it isn't verdict-free.
- **Hopeful:** the player's ability stayed out, OC-9 was the most sensible reply of any batch, and h7 read its consequence line as the corpus does.
