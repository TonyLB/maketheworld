**What the batch says (mine, a reading of 15 calls).** The contract sentence states motion in relational terms: "whose relations change, or that move relative to the thing they are on or in". That helps where the glue and the physics agree. On OC-30/2, the teapot moves with the table because it is glued, and the tip-with went from 1 of 3 to 3 of 3. It hurts where they conflict. On OC-6, pouring needs the teapot to move relative to the table, and the replies reasoned "glued, so stationary relative to the table". So the same sentence looks like the cause of both changes: it invites the reply to settle each thing's motion from its relations, and the glue is a relation. That is PR-1, the motion written to fit the rule, brought back by wording. The form gain and the silence look separable from it (PB-L11).
