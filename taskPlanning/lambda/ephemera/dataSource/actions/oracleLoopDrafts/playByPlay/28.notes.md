## Why

`consistent` is read as "the rule is still in force", a status report on the rule, so rules get hung on consequences they don't bear on ([20.notes.md](20.notes.md#diagnosis-with-the-user-2026-11-19-why-a-consistent-rule-at-all)). The user's reframing (2026-11-20):
- **No value is needed for "this doesn't break the rule".** A violation can be proposed, and the appropriate authority rules it down.
- **The value that is needed says "this expresses the rule in action":** the rule brings about a result that ordinarily wouldn't happen, like a teapot not sliding off a tilted table.

The stance becomes `effect`, rendered `Rule (Effect)`, and the ask says what each value judges (draft 20's cause 3).

## What changed from draft 26

- **The code** (IS-14):
  - `RULE_STANCES` becomes `violation` and `effect`, and the render word becomes `Rule (Effect)`.
  - A reply's `"consistent"` is refused: "stance" must be one of violation, effect.
  - The cells' inputs hashes don't move, since no input carries the stance's word.
- **The crate example's JSON:** `"stance": "consistent"` becomes `"stance": "effect"`. This is a genuine effect: [lamp1] rises with [crate1] because it's bolted on.
- **The Task** (the wording mine): "…and "stance", "violation" or "consistent"." becomes "…and "stance": "violation" when the consequence breaks the rule, or "effect" when the rule makes the consequence happen where it ordinarily wouldn't."
- **Unchanged:** the Role, including its "and to the scene's rules" (cause 1, the next slice), and every other example.
