## Why

- **The Role's "and to the scene's rules"** (draft 20's cause 1) asks what happens *to* a rule, as if it had a fate to report. What it was reaching for is the three things a step can do with a rule: bring about the rule's effect, go against it, or end it.
- **The third had no value.** A step aimed at ending a rule, like prying the glued teapot loose with a claw hammer, could only be tagged `Violation`. OC-19's replies have done exactly that in every batch.
- **The user (2026-11-20):** a third stance, `voids` (not "removed", which could be confused with removing physical objects), "a necessary adjustment to address the question of what "and to the scene's rules" is trying to express".
- **The house already uses the word:** it matches the corpus's `CandidateStep.voids` and the parent's JP-14 ("a direct attempt on a rule").

## What changed from draft 28 (the wording mine)

- **The code** (the `voids` stance):
  - `RULE_STANCES` becomes `violation`, `effect`, `voids`, rendered `Rule (Voids)`.
  - The inputs hashes are unchanged.
  - The after-state (a confirmed void's rule gone for later steps) is booked on the parent's "State carried forward".
- **The Role:**
  - Was: "What the step brings about beyond its aim, to other things and to the scene's rules, is checked by others, so offer it freely as a consequence…".
  - Now: "What the step brings about beyond its aim is checked by others: what happens to other things, what a scene's rule makes happen, a rule it goes against, or a rule it ends. So offer it freely as a consequence…".
- **The Task's stance sentence:**
  - Was: ""violation" when the consequence breaks the rule, or "effect" when…".
  - Now: ""violation" when the consequence goes against a rule that stays in force, "effect" when the rule makes the consequence happen where it ordinarily wouldn't, and "voids" when the consequence ends the rule."
- **A new boat example,** after the cart: "cut [boat1] free from [post2] with [knife1]". The rope is sawn through, "[boat1] is no longer tied to [post2]", `voids` [boat-tied-to-post]. It is an analog of OC-19 with a different relation and tool. Each value now has one example: the crate's lamp an `effect`, the cart's pull a `violation`, the boat's rope a `voids`.
- **A caveat:** the three ask changes are one change in meaning, so a mixed result is read per cell, not per change.
