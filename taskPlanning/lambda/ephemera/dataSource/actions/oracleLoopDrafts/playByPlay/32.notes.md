**A mechanism for OC-1 (mine, from the render):** the scene's teapot line says "[teapot1] is glued to [table1] (rule [teapot-secured-to-table]), so it goes wherever [table1] goes". The render itself states that the rule causes the carry. Tagging the teapot's carry `causes` is faithful to that sentence, and the lesson's "even when a rule holds the thing on" lost to it. The saucer's line ("When [table1] moves, [saucer1] moves with it") then gets a parallel rule by analogy, which is why the invented handle appears only beside the teapot's. The render's carry wording is booked on the imagination plan; this is the strongest evidence yet that it drives the stray.

**Verdict (mine, for review):**
- **Draft 32 is the best result since draft 28: the prompt to beat** (the user: a benchmark the other arm is compared against, not a base it is built on). Form 38 against 33, the grip slot emptied where there was nothing to say, status strays gone, OC-1b and OC-6/command held or better.
