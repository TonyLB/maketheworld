## Why

- **The merge no longer needs it.** A revised step keeps its children, unchecked and marked stale (the revision rule, calibration's [The action plan](../../../../../../../lambda/ephemera/calibration/AGENT.md#the-action-plan)). A child given back is a repeat, and only the child's authority clears its mark. So "give again each of its items you keep" asks for work the merge drops.
- **The view hides what it asked for.** `playByPlay` reads consequences only once confirmed. The coat example showed an unchecked consequence in its input, which the piece is now never shown.

## The change

Three cuts, one idea (the ask matches the revision rule):
- **The Task:** "When you revise the step, give again each of its items you keep, as a consequence of the step or of the sub-step that brings it about." is gone.
- **The coat's lesson:** now "When the step names its things loosely, give it revised to name them." The clause on giving held consequences again is gone.
- **The coat's input:** "The plan holds an unchecked consequence: [hat1] falls off [hook2]." is gone. The answer is unchanged, so "[hat1] falls off [hook2]" is now the reply's own offer, on the sub-step that brings it about.
