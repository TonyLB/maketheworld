## Why

- **`effect` reads as any result.** Draft 29a's strays tag "[tea2] flows from [teapot1] into [cup1]" and the grip's "[table1] is lifted", 14 in 48 ([29a.notes.md](29a.notes.md#results-2026-11-20-batches-54-and-54b)). The word's ordinary sense is the action's result.
- **The user's rename:** `causes`, the rule causing the consequence. A stray now reads as plainly false: "the glue causes [tea2] to flow".
- **The crate example didn't meet the criterion** (the user, 2026-11-20: "Wouldn't the lamp normally rise when the crate is lifted?"). A lamp resting on a lifted crate rises too, so "[lamp1] rises with [crate1]" was an ordinary carry tagged with the rule. It's been in every ask since draft 20, the only non-violation rule example. It taught the most persistent stray: a carry tagged with the securing (the saucer rising with the table, with the glue or an invented rule).
- **The two go together.** A `causes` on a carry would teach the old reading under the new name.

## What changed from draft 28 (the wording mine)

- **The code** (the `causes` rename):
  - `RULE_STANCES` becomes `violation`, `causes`, `voids`, rendered `Rule (Causes)`.
  - OC-1's look-for now says a `Rule (Causes)` belongs only where the glue changes the result, and on an ordinary carry it's a stray.
  - Inputs hashes unchanged.
- **The crate example:** "Step: turn [crate1] over, with the intent [crate1] is upside down. [lamp1] is bolted to [crate1] (rule [lamp-secured-to-crate]). The detail asked for: how [crate1] is turned." The sub-steps are "grip [crate1] by its sides" and "roll [crate1] over onto its top", with "[lamp1] stays fixed to [crate1], upside down with it" as `causes`. A lamp resting on the crate would fall off as it turned, and the bolt is what keeps it on.
- **The Task's stance sentence:** "…or "effect" when the rule makes the consequence happen where it ordinarily wouldn't." becomes "…or "causes" when the rule causes the consequence, a result that ordinarily wouldn't happen."
- **Unchanged:** the Role (draft 28's), the other examples. No `voids` and no boat in the ask.
