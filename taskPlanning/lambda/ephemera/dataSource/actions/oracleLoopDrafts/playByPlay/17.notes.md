## Rubric (written before the batch; mine until reviewed)

**Form and content are graded apart,** as `consequences`' draft 3 did.

**Form: does the block merge?** The check is `playByPlayContract`. It is recorded and not held to a bar on a first draft. A miss is recorded by its cause:
- no block, or the reply cut off (PB-P6);
- the old `actions` block, or a `problems` list;
- a single sub-step;
- an entry key other than `says` and `intent` (a `thing`, say);
- a consequence or concern;
- an empty `changes`;
- an expansion and a mark together;
- a written `to`.

**Content: parity with draft 16** (batch 31), on step 0's look-fors, read from each reply and its merged plan. A reply that fails the form is still read for content, from its list.

**Against parity:**

**Recorded, not graded** (step 0's "recorded across cells", plus mine):
