## What changed from draft 21

**One example, added before the rope** (the direction is the user's, 2026-11-19, to tune for the concession cell next; the example is mine):

> Step: open [jar1] (with the intent open [jar1]). The plan already holds, unchecked: [jar1] is stuck shut. The detail asked for: how [jar1] is opened.
> - hold [jar1] in one hand: [jar1] is held steady
> - twist the lid of [jar1] with the other hand: the lid turns and comes off

**What it shows:** a claim already in the plan, unchecked, against the step, answered with the ordinary method. The claim is neither restated nor worked around.
- **No scene rule:** the claim is a bare assertion, so the lesson is about the unchecked register, not about rules. That keeps it away from OC-20's shape: no securing, no lift.
- **No consequences.** With it, the examples hold 6, of which 2 have consequences with a rule, 2 have consequences with none, and 2 have none.

**The risks (mine):**
- **Over-correction into ignoring what the plan holds.** That would cost watch item 2's collaboration. This draft doesn't test that, and it's recorded only as a risk.
- **The intent "the lid turns and comes off"** settles that the lid comes off, against the "stuck shut" claim. That's the method's aim, in IS-8's terms, not a check of the claim, but a reader could take it as the example refuting the claim. If concession replies start contradicting the claim in their intents ("[table1] is lifted despite the chain"), that's this example's doing.
- **Copying:** "twist", "stuck", "held steady".

**A second change, the user's (2026-11-19): every example's step line brackets its intent.** "open [jar1], with the intent open [jar1]" can read as one run-on instruction, so the line now reads "open [jar1] (with the intent open [jar1])". It is applied to all six examples, so the jar doesn't stand apart (mine). It is a form change on every example, not aimed at the concession cell, so any movement on the other cells may be its doing.

**Unchanged:** the Role, the Task, and the other examples' content.

**Checked** (mine): each of the six examples' blocks passes `playByPlayContract`. The cells and inputs hashes are as in batches 35 to 37.
