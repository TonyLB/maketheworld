# Non-rule cell draft 1: notes and reading rubric

For [the non-rule cell plan](../../AGENT.nonRuleCell.planning.md#recommended-order)'s step 2, the baseline. Draft 1 is mine, modelled on the rule cell's draft 3 ([`../ruleCell/3.txt`](../ruleCell/3.txt)): describe first, then the block (RC-L5, RC-L6).

## The draft

[`1.txt`](1.txt). Its parts:
- **Role** and **Reading the scene**, as the rule cell's.
- **Reading the steps:**
  - what the three roles mean;
  - that each step happens in the state the earlier ones leave;
  - that securing is judged separately, put positively as what this piece judges (size, weight, reach, position, what earlier steps did).
- **Task:** NR-1's question; a walk over the steps in order, a sentence each, stopping at the first step that blocks or can't be told; then NR-4's three block shapes.

The role words (`intended`, `method`, `consequence`) are the render's, and are a draft lever. The `reason` asks for "what stops it" plainly. Wording it as the brief for an alternate method or a mitigation (NR-2) is a later draft, once the baseline shows what Micro writes unprompted.

## How it is probed

All eleven cells, `repeats: 3`, `NovaMicro`, no options. That is 33 calls, over the cap of 24, so the batch is two payloads:
- **1a,** the eight single-step cells (OC-11, OC-18, OC-3, OC-1, OC-2, OC-20, OC-6, OC-9): 24 calls;
- **1b,** the three OC-30 hops (h5, h7, h10): 9 calls.

The grade is the piece's own (NR-4's check), giving `allows`, `blocks <step>/<cause>` or `needs detail of <of> <step>`. OC-9 is tallied and not graded (JF-2).

## Rubric (written before the batch)

- **The tally,** per cell, against its expected answer. The bar for a baseline is none; the batch says where the drafts start.
- **Over-asking and under-asking:**
  - a `needs detail` on any cell but OC-30/h5 is over-asking, noting which `of`;
  - a verdict on h5 is under-asking. An `allows` on h5 means the walk assumed the cup stays put; a `blocks` means it assumed the slide.
- **The rules set aside:** on OC-1, OC-20 and OC-6, does the walk raise the glue or the chain? A `blocks` that cites it is the known false refusal in this piece's form.
- **`reason` and `missing`,** read against each cell's `lookFor`:
  - on the size cells, the size, and on OC-3 not the cup's being on the table;
  - on h7, whether the reason names the cup off the spout because of the slide, which is what a mitigation would need;
  - on h5, whether `missing` asks what the tip does to the saucer and cup.
- **The walk over three steps** (NR-4's risk):
  - on the OC-30 hops, does the prose take each step in order and stop at the right one?
  - on h7, does it reach step 3 and blame step 2, or stop at step 2 (`blocks 2/2`, the slide as if it couldn't happen)?
- **Form:** each malformed reply's reason, especially quotes that match no step (the role label quoted with the step, a paraphrase, or the request quoted in place of a step).
- **Length:** a reply cut off at 512 tokens shows as "no JSON block". If any are, the cap is a lever (an answered piece has no `maxTokens` yet).
- **OC-9:** which answer the trestle gets, and whether the reasoning reaches for weight and effort, as background for the skill-or-chance outcome (step 5).
