# Non-rule cell draft 5: notes and reading rubric

For [the non-rule cell plan](../../AGENT.nonRuleCell.planning.md#recommended-order)'s step 3, drafts in isolation: library entries **NR-L8** (a tagged factor list before the finding) and **NR-L9** (`cause` optional) in [`iterations.md`](iterations.md). The design is the plan's [Draft 5's proposal](../../AGENT.nonRuleCell.planning.md#draft-5s-proposal-a-tagged-factor-list-with-cause-made-optional), accepted by the user 2026-11-13.

## Why

Batch 3's replies give the finding first and then fit a reason to it (the user's diagnosis). Draft 4 put "for and against" ahead of the finding. The weighing took, but the two buckets were filled: the "for" side with filler, and the "against" side by hunting, which found the securing ([4.notes.md](4.notes.md#batch-4-overall)). Draft 5 asks for the factors that bear on the step, each tagged, with no quota per tag. Separately, `cause` on a self-blamed step was malformed in 12 replies in batch 3 and 22 in batch 4 (NR-P7), after two wordings.

## The change

Built from [`3.txt`](3.txt), since draft 4 wasn't kept. Two changes, an agreed exception to one change per draft, since each shows in its own measure:
- **The factor list (NR-L8):** a paragraph before the walk. A factor is something stated (by the scene, the steps before, or the step) or something the step depends on that nothing states. Each is written first, then tagged For, Against, Effect unclear or Not given. The list has no quota, and the finding is weighed from it. The walk's sentence orders each step as its description, then its factors, then its finding and state. Each JSON entry gains `"factors"`, as `"<factor>: <tag>"`, ahead of `finding`. The block is "a single" JSON code block.
- **`cause` optional (NR-L9):** the blocked shape drops `cause`, and a sentence adds it back "when the trouble comes from an earlier step or from a consequence line". The grade reads an absent `cause`, or one quoting nothing given, as the step itself.
- **The cap:** `maxTokens` 1536 (from 1024). The cap is a setting on the piece, so no hash changes.

**Unchanged:** every cell's inputs, so every inputs hash matches batches 3 and 4. Also unchanged: draft 3's findings, its state paragraph, and its other sections, the securing sentence included.

- **The hops:**

  - h7: does the pour blame `1c1`?

- **Invented Not given factors** (the user's, 2026-11-13): Not given factors that a plain reading of the step doesn't need ("whether the player is strong enough", "whether the cup is clean"). Count them per cell, and note which push a finding to `needs detail`. Naming unstated factors may bring NR-P2's preconditions back under a new tag. Finding the balance between eliciting real gaps and inventing new ones is expected to take more than one draft.
- **Filler:** For factors that restate the step ("the player can reach it"), and lists padded to fill all four tags.
- **Order:** does the prose give a finding before the step's factors? Does a finding contradict the step's own tags (all For, yet blocked)?
- **Tag form:** factors with the tag missing, put first, or not one of the four. Is the prose list the same as the block's?
- **OC-9:** what its factors call the trestle's weight ("difficult" under Against or Not given). This is evidence for the skill-or-chance finding.
- **Form:**
  - NR-P7: a `cause` present on a self-blamed step, now read as the step. Count it, and say whether it quotes the step, a reason, or a scene fact;
  - parse failures (per-step blocks);
  - `of` left out;
  - replies cut off at 1536;
  - reply lengths against batches 3 and 4.

- **The securing, by tag:** the glue or the chain is Against in every reply where the scene has it (9 of 9), and is also Effect unclear in OC-1 and OC-6. The Effect unclear tag didn't take the glue off the Against side. It added a second, vaguer factor beside it.

- **Filler:** For items that restate the step or its context ("player has intention", "player has free hands", "cup is accessible") in 8 of 11.
- **Order:** the prose gives the factors before the finding in every reply. The finding follows the tags: an Against or a Not given decides it whenever there is one.
- **Tag form:** **the tags are read as headings, in every reply.** Eight replies group the prose by tag (a `**For:**` heading, then items under it). The three hops write one line per tag ("- For: …"). That is 0 of 11 with the factor first. All four tags are filled in 8 of 11 replies, each with one or two items. In the block, `factors` is a list of objects in all 11, `{"factor": …, "tag": …}` or `{"<factor>": "<tag>"}`, never the asked-for strings. The factor still comes before its tag there.
- **OC-9:** "player capability", Not given.
- **Form:** NR-P7 is gone: **no reply gives `cause`**, so nothing is malformed or read as the step (0 against batch 3's 12). No parse failures, though h5 and h10 write a block per step and then the whole one. That works only because the whole block is last. No `of` left out, no reply cut off. Lengths are 1860 to 2468 chars for one step, 2864 to 4178 for the hops. h5's 4178 is near 1024 tokens, so the old cap may have cut it.

**What it shows:**
- **Four tags in a list are four buckets** (mine). This is draft 4's failure with two more buckets: each tag becomes a heading, and each heading gets filled. "Write each factor, then its tag" was not followed once in the prose.
- **Not given is the new hunting ground.** It gathers the player's ability and the objects' unstated physics: NR-P2's invented preconditions under a tag that licenses them. It decides most of the batch's wrong answers. Much of what it gathers (ability, strength, force) is the booked skill-or-chance finding's territory, and OC-9 says so directly.
- **Effect unclear didn't work as hoped.** It took "mechanics of pouring from a glued teapot" and "glue strength", vague doubts beside an Against that still decided, not a route to `consequences`.
- **`cause` optional works** (NR-L9): no `cause` was given on any self-blamed step, and no answer was hidden. It is worth keeping on its own.
- **Not kept** (mine, pending the user): the factor list as drafted costs the guards their gains. The `cause` change is separable and kept.
