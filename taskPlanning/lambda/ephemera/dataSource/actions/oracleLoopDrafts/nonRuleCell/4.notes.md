From draft 3, the base (kept at the batch 3 checkpoint, user, 2026-11-13).

## Why

Batch 3's replies give each step's verdict first ("**Finding:** It is blocked", then "**Reason:**"), and the reasons fit the verdict rather than weigh toward it (the user's reading, 2026-11-13, of a pattern that has bitten before: RC-P2). Examples:
- h7 r1 blocks the tip because the table is "small and round, making it unstable to tip over";
- h7 r2 and r3 block it for want of information, a needs-detail reason on a blocked finding;
- OC-1 calls the lift "difficult", under a verdict of impossible;
- h10 invents "glued to the floor".

Draft 3 asked for a description, then the finding. But the three findings are listed as labels before the walk, and the JSON puts `finding` before everything but `step`, so the replies led with the label.

## The change

Two changes, combined as a stated exception to one change per draft (user, 2026-11-13). Each shows in its own measure: the first in the findings, the second only in malformed `cause`s on single-step cells.
1. **For and against, before the finding** (the user's "balancing the evidence"; "for and against" the user's; wording mine). The walk now says, for each step, what in the state bears on it, "both what lets it happen and what stands against it", then "weigh them and give its finding". Each JSON entry gains `"for and against"` ahead of `finding`, so the reasoning has a structured place before the verdict, as `establishes` gave success one in draft 3. It isn't a list of only what might stand in the way: that was draft 1's hunt checklist. The grade ignores the field (fields no finding has are ignored), and it is read by hand.
   - `establishes` stays what the step leaves, after the finding. It is what happens, not the evidence for whether it can (the user, 2026-11-13, correcting my first proposal).
2. **The `cause` wording (NR-P7):** "the step or consequence line above that is to blame, quoted exactly; this step itself if nothing before it is to blame", in place of draft 3's "… quoted; the same step if nothing before it is to blame".

Also: the second "Then" before the JSON became "Last", since the walk now has a "Then" of its own.

**Unchanged:** the findings and their framing, the cells, the render, the grade. Inputs hashes match batch 3's, h7 included.

## Rubric (written before the batch)

- **NR-P7:** malformed `cause`s, against batch 3's 12. The bar is none.
- **The order of the prose:** per step, does it weigh before it finds? Count the steps whose prose gives the finding first.
- **For and against:** does it hold both sides, or is it a list of obstacles (the hunt checklist again) or of permissions only? Do the findings follow from it: an obstacle named and weighed as decisive for a block, none decisive for works?
- **Reasons that don't fit their finding:** a needs-detail reason under `blocked`, "difficult" under impossible, a reason that argues the other way (h7 r1's "unstable"). Batch 3 had several; count them.
- **The guards:** `allows` on OC-1, OC-2, OC-20, OC-6 and h10, against batch 3's 6 of 15 in substance. Do OC-1 and OC-20 weigh the glue and chain and come out for works, or does the securing still decide?
- **The tip (NR-P3):** on h5, h7 and h10, is the tip still "not feasible"? With the for and against, what is named against it?
- **The size cells must hold,** OC-11, OC-18 and OC-3 `blocks 1/1`, and with the size as the reason. Batch 3 had the size in 5 of 9.
- **h7:** if the tip works, does the pour blame the consequence line (`blocks 2/1c1`)?
- **NR-P8:** a blocked step's `establishes`, the impossible outcome or the unchanged state?
- **Form:** malformed for any other reason, and replies cut off at 1024 tokens, since each entry now carries more.
- **OC-9:** what the for and against says of the trestle.
