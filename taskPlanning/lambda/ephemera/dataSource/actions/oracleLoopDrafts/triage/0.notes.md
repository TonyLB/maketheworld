Form: 8 of 12 fail in 0a, 2 of 12 in 0b.

**What it answers**
- **Implied tools come out of the same expansion as the teapot** (the user's point): 0a gave one in `cut`, `trim`, `wipe` and both lights, as readily as for the tools the player named.
- **The arms miss on opposite sides.** 0a improvises needlessly in 4 of 12 (three hands, a spout). 0b never does, and in the 4 tavern cells that need a tool it takes the hammer, which is no blade and no flame. By the uneven costs (JP-13) 0b's miss is the worse: a needless improvisation is a needless defer, where a wrong tool the scene has may be a wrong "yes".
- **The hearth is used by neither.**
- **Two cups:** 0b chose one, against the line. 0a's cup is no evidence, since it grounds almost nothing.

**Mechanisms**
- **0a, the hands:** the Role's "what it is done with" is a slot, and with nothing else to put there Micro gives the hand ([every slot gets filled](../../../../../../../lambda/ephemera/calibration/AGENT.md#2-every-slot-gets-filled)).
- **0a, the form:** nothing shows the block's shape. "Each thing it uses named by its handle" became a `uses` list (4), and "the object starts with" became `"says": "improvise"` and `list` (4). Grounding went into those slots, so `says` names a scene handle in 2 of 12.
- **0b, the hammer:** the two lessons choose on whether the scene *has* something, and never on whether it suits. The found example's scene holds a thing that suits, and the improvised one's holds no tool at all, so a tool at hand is always the found case ([an unconditioned option](../../../../../../../lambda/ephemera/calibration/AGENT.md#6-decisions-and-their-defaults)).
- **0b, the places:** "from [hook1]" in the found example is copied as where a thing is ("from [saucer1] on [table1]").
- **[tea2] in neither,** 0 of 4: naming the source stands in for grounding the tea.

**My predictions:** right on the tea, the cloths, the hearth unused, `take`, and 0b choosing a cup. **Wrong** on the rest that matter: 0a improvised tools freely where I expected nothing, 0b used the hammer where I expected a blade or a match, and 0a named no handle for the teapot.

**What the form check can't see:** [hammer1] passes it. So does an improvised thing no text names.
