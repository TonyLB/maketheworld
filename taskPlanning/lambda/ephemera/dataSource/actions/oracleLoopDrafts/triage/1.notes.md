### Mechanisms (mine)

1. **"Defer" goes with words left ungrounded.** `says` with a scene thing left in words, wholly or in part, by branch, over both arms' well-formed replies: Defer 9 of 17, Yes 4 of 27, No 2 of 9. The window is the one example whose `says` names no handle, and it is a "Defer", and its lesson ("they stay in the player's words") is quoted by T-23 1a for a name that fits one thing. Once the reply has decided "Defer", the step is copied as written: [the cheapest branch](../../../../../../../lambda/ephemera/calibration/AGENT.md#6-decisions-and-their-defaults). **This is grounding and branching spoiling each other,** the risk the plan names. And it reaches the guard: T-12 1a, "pick up the table" in words, keeps its wrong "No".
2. **`Here` takes what is at hand.** With [hammer1] held, `Here` names it for something sharp, for cutting and for smashing, 6 of 6, and it suits only the smashing. With nothing held, `Here: none` (T-13, T-14). T-21 1a takes [cup1] to light a candle. The fern example, holding a spade with `Here: none`, didn't transfer. `Here` asks for "the thing in the scene that ordinarily does that", and the scene's nearest thing fills it. 0d's line didn't add a test of suiting, and neither did 0c's example.
3. **`Here: none` isn't followed by improvising** when the player names the tool (T-14 2 of 2: "wipe the table with a cloth", in words). T-13 improvised its screwdriver 2 of 2. So the block doesn't always do what the line decides.
4. **The pour isn't pictured as a tilt.** All six pours are "Yes", the glue never weighed. 1b T-10 says the teapot "remains stationary"; 1a's steps take it "from [table1]". **The guard caught all six**, since every one names [teapot1]. It is the case the guard is for.

The cart lesson ("moves something farther than a rule allows") and the crate's ("Yes even when a rule holds the thing on") don't tell apart a host moving its glued child and a child moved off it.
6. **The chain is taken as permanent,** 2 of 2 on T-12, the case the user flagged for the voiding part (TI-7). Neither reply quotes "can be moved as usual".
7. **The ambiguity lesson lands on a name, not on two matches.** T-23's "china cup" is deferred, 2 of 2; T-24's "the cup", which fits both cups, is grounded on [cup1], 2 of 2, which is wrong after the guard. 0b chose a cup too.
