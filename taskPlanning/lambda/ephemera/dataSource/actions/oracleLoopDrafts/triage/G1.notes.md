## The piece

`triageGrounding` ([`triageGrounding.ts`](../../../../../../../lambda/ephemera/calibration/oraclePieces/triageGrounding.ts)), designed with the user and built 2026-11-27. The grounding gives no verdict, so its answer is the grounding itself; what the guard makes of it is the split's.
- **The view:** triage's cells, the scene with its securing omitted, in the explicit render. Where a scene has no securing its render is triage's, and the pins say so.
- **The contract:** triage's without the verdict: `match`, then the step record, merged as triage merges it. A `kind` or `reason` refuses the reply.
- **The answer:** the grounding, in the corpus's form ("coffee1, spoon1", "cup1|cup2", "rope1, improvised"), against the row's accepted groundings, any of which is right, on **every** row (the user: a grounding does its work whatever another arm makes of it; T-15 and T-21, open only on their branch, too, mine). Over-naming is wrong. `matched` and `added` tallied beside it.
- **The platform:** a cell may expect a list of accepted answers, where its piece declares it (`OraclePiece`'s third type parameter); every other piece keeps its single string.
- **Max tokens:** triage's 1,024.
- **`combine-triage`** reads a `triageGrounding` batch by its own contract and render, and now counts the grounding right on every row. On that count, draft 5's rerun grounding, the stand-in, is **14 of 27** (10 of 21 under the old count).

**Checked:** every example's block through the contract (7 of 7 accepted); both payloads through the handler with a mocked model, accepted, every cell covered once, each row's own grounding graded right (T-24 aside, where the mock, matching cells by command, answered with T-17's, the same command; the piece's own test grades T-24 right).
