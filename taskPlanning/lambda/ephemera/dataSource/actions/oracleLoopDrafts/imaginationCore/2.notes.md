## What `consequences` draft 1 adds of its own (mine)

- **A Role:** "the way the action … will be done has been chosen"; "what happens to the things in the scene as that action is done"; "point out any of those consequences that work against what the player intends".
- **Examples, each with an intent,** since its problems are those against the intent:
  - the book set on a shelf, with its bookmark;
  - a cloth pulled off a table, knocking a figurine to the floor, with no problem: collateral is listed, not flagged;
  - the crate's carry;
  - the rug's dropped crate.
- **A departure from step 3's list:** the rug example's intent is "to sweep the floor under the rug". That makes its dropped crate the one example where a consequence works against the intent, so the problems list has a model. Step 3 didn't specify one.
- **A Task:** "Write the consequences …"; "how each of the things after "The things:" moves as the action happens" (PB-L22); "the possible problems: any of those consequences that works against the intent, the line starting "The intent"".
