**What it shows.**

- **The examples were copied as the answers.** Every reply in every case wrote the two lines from the example's two phrases, "lifted and tilted" and "stays where it is", assigned in some order, whatever `Picture` said. OC-1 gave "teapot stays, table lifted and tilted" 3 of 3, against a `Picture` that lifts the teapot with the table, and OC-30/2 the same 3 of 3. An example value in a form is read by Micro as the answer's vocabulary. That holds for Micro prompts generally, so it goes to Writing prompts for Nova Micro when the episode closes.
- **The branches still aren't read from the lines.** Only OC-6/1 wrote lines matching `Picture`, and it then answered `apart: no`. In 12 replies, no branch set follows from its two lines.
- **The verdict came loose from `apart`.** OC-6/2 and /3 answered `apart: yes`, then `keeps`. Draft 2's verdict followed `apart` in every reply. The added sentence put more between the question and the verdict.
