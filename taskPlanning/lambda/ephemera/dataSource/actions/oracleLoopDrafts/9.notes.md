## The change

The `Rules` step stops restating the rule's logic (the user's question, 2026-11-09: are we hard-coding `apart` into how the prompt treats every rule?). Since draft 2 the step asked the violation form's three clauses back as fixed questions, `together`, `changes first` and `apart`, and tied the verdict to `apart`, which assumes every rule is a two-thing securing. The rule's own text, generated per kind of relation, already states what breaks it. Draft 2 changed both together (L-2), and which half did the work was never tested.

The step now reads: "For each, write the rule's handle. Then, from what Picture said, write each thing the rule names with what it does, in the form "[x]: what Picture says [x] does". Then, given those lines, write "breaks" if what they describe is what the rule says breaks it, otherwise "keeps"." The lines from `Picture` stay (draft 4's grounding), worded for any number of things, not "the two things". Nothing else changes; `Tests`' "Does any rule say 'breaks'?" is left as it is (L-20).

The diagnosis is the user's; the wording mine.
