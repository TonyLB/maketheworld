## Rubric (written before the batch)

There is no earlier batch: this is the baseline. For comparison, the play-by-play's OC-30/2 had the knock-on 0 of 3 in batch 11 (draft 10), where the saucer was given a part in the plan, and 2 of 3 at best (batch 3).

| Cell | Look for (mine until reviewed) | Partly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OC-30/2 | The saucer and cup slide off the tipped table, and the cup no longer under the spout is a problem against the intent | The slide without the problem; or the problem flagged without the slide (a generic "the tea may miss") |
| OC-1, the control | Everything on the table rises with it, and nothing works against the intent | The carry, with a flag that doesn't work against picking up the table (things that might slide off, the glue) |

**What else to record:**
- **Keeping the method succeeding:** whether a reply questions or changes the chosen action, such as the table not tipping, the glue stopping it, or a step added. That isn't this ask's job.
- **Collateral flagged, or listed:** on OC-1, a slide or fall flagged as a problem goes against the "listed, not flagged" premise. It costs an oracle call, not a wrong answer.
- **The watched core sentence:** "you don't need to show that it survives". Does a reply leave a thing silent, or say it "stays", where it moves? A miss on OC-30/2's saucer that reads as the fact surviving points at the core (exit (c)).
- **Silent things:** a listed thing with no line stays in its frame.
  - On OC-1, the frame is the table's, which rises, so silence is the carry.
  - On OC-30/2, a silent saucer stays in the tipped table's frame, which is the opposite of the slide, so silence there is a miss.
- **Form:**
  - a line for a thing not listed, such as the table, which the action names;
  - `actions` as sentences, or one entry per thing (PB-P1);
  - the `problems` key.
- **Grain and length;** the cap is 1024 (PB-P6).
