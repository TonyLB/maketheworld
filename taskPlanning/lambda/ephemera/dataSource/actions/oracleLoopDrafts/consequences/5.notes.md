### Rubric (written before the batch; mine until reviewed)

**A hedge, which is counted.** Draft 4 has no possibilities, so a hedge shows in the merged plan as either:
- **a conditional `says`:** "may", "might", "could", "if", "unless", "risk", or the like;
- **any concern.** Both cells have nothing to work against the intent.

**The bar is 0 of 3 hedged replies on each cell.** It is recorded here and graded again at draft 5.

**Recorded, not counted:**
- prose hedges outside the block;
- hedges in a refused block or a stray key, as in batch 4's `"concerns"`.

**Content:**
- **`cup`:**
  - [tea1] rises with [cup1], 3 of 3;
  - [saucer1] stays on [table1], 3 of 3.
- **`OC-1/teapot1`:** [teapot1] rises with [table1], 3 of 3.

**Form:** does the block merge? Record the cause of any miss:
- an unlisted thing (CQ-P4), for example [cup1] on `cup`, or [table1] on either;
- a written `to`;
- a stray key.

**Also record:**
- **the glue doubted** (`OC-1/teapot1`), as in "unless securely glued", or "the glue may give";
- **the spill line echoed** (`cup`): does "unless the motion spills it out" come back as a condition?
- **the saucer moved** (`cup`): does it lift with the cup, or rattle?
- **length.**
