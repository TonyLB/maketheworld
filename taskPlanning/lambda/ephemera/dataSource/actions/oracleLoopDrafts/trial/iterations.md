# The trial: iterations

The drafts of the trial's three pieces, `trialAgainst`, `trialRebuttal` and `trialVerdict`, for [the non-rule cell plan](../../AGENT.nonRuleCell.planning.md#recommended-order)'s step 3. The design is [the trial proposal](../../AGENT.nonRuleCell.planning.md#the-trial-proposal-argue-against-rebut-judge). Files are named by piece and number (`against.1.txt`). Problems and Library entries are numbered `TR-Pn` and `TR-Ln`. The cells and inputs are the non-rule cell's, so problems shared with it keep their `NR-` ids.
