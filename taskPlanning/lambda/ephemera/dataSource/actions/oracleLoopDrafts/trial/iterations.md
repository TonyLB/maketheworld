# The trial: iterations

The drafts of the trial's three stages, `against`, `rebuttal` and `verdict`, for [the non-rule cell plan](../../AGENT.nonRuleCell.planning.md#recommended-order)'s step 3. The design is [the trial proposal](../../AGENT.nonRuleCell.planning.md#the-trial-proposal-argue-against-rebut-judge). The stages are one harness kind, `trial`, from Slice A (2026-11-16; [the family](../../AGENT.nonRuleCell.planning.md#the-trial-family-one-harness-kind-the-stage-by-specifier)): a payload says `"piece": "trial"` and names its stage in `options.stage`. Batches 1 to 8 ran as the separate pieces `trialAgainst` and `trialRebuttal`, with the same renders (pinned by test). The verdict, from Slice B ([its design](../../AGENT.nonRuleCell.planning.md#slice-bs-design-the-verdict-and-the-stage-contract)), is an answered stage graded by code, on lifted pairs: a batch 8 rebuttal shown with the argument it answered. Files are named by stage and number (`against.1.txt`). The `reading.N` files (`reading.1s.txt` and `reading.1e.txt`, one per arm) are the [trial question plan](../../AGENT.trialQuestion.planning.md)'s step 0 probe, the piece `sceneReading`: one question asked of a scene alone, in the judge render with its guide or in the explicit render with none. Problems and Library entries are numbered `TR-Pn` and `TR-Ln`. The cells and inputs are the non-rule cell's, so problems shared with it keep their `NR-` ids.
