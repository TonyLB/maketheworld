# Qualified referents: "get the cup on the table"

**Status: opened 2026-10-23; QR-1, QR-3, QR-4, QR-6, QR-7 and QR-8 decided by the user the same day. Repointed 2026-10-23 to one iteration: a qualified take on the deterministic fast path. Stacked phrases and `put` moved to [the shelf](#not-in-scope). Slices 0, 1 and 2 are done (the harness, the Parse baseline, the worked cases and the decisions; the dry run judging an assertion; grouping and lowering, so the LLM route's qualified takes work). **Next step: Slice 3, the fast path splits.** An implementation plan, per [`taskPlanning/AGENT.md`](../../../../AGENT.md), with a corpus-first opening like positionReferents'. It is rung 6 of the iteration ladder in [`AGENT.objectManipulationIterations.planning.md`](AGENT.objectManipulationIterations.planning.md), and it supersedes that rung's earlier plan (deterministic fast-path improvements, BD-24, deleted 2026-10-23); what it kept from BD-24 is in [What this replaces](#what-this-replaces).

## Goal

Let a take name **which** object, or which part of one, by **where it is**: "get the cup on the table", "get the cloth on the table", "get bag from table". Read it on the deterministic fast path, with no Bedrock call.

**Payoff, stated at the observable output:**

- Two cups, one on the table and one on the floor: `get the cup on the table` picks up that cup without asking.
- A cloth draped over a table: `get the cloth on the table` takes the table part without asking (today it can only be reached by being asked).
- One bag, on the floor: `get bag from table` is refused with a line that says why.

**Scope (user, 2026-10-23).** The target is the deterministic path. The fast path already claims exactly the verbs a single qualifier serves (take, get, drop). The stages after the flat skeleton (grouping, lowering the qualifier, the arity gate, narrowing) do not care which route built it, so the LLM route's qualified takes work too, at no extra cost. A drop's tail is not read (QR-7). Stacked phrases, bracketings and `put` are shelved under "Shelved: stacked qualifiers and `put`" in [`actions/AGENT.implementation.md`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/AGENT.implementation.md).

## Why now

The seed is the shelf "Shelved: asking the player" in [`actions/AGENT.implementation.md`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/AGENT.implementation.md), left by positionReferents (retired 2026-10-21). Three of its bullets stop at the same missing capability:

- **Naming a position by phrase.** A part of a spread object is reached only by being asked. The bullet names the parse question (a phrase such as "on the table" competes with the destination reading of the same words) and says a label source that qualifies by position is the shared fix for two same-named cups too.
- **Option labels for same-named objects.** An object with no parent to name reads by its bare name "until qualified referents (`{ objectSpan, qualified }`) exist".
- **One object under two keys is never a candidate.** It waits for a command that names two parts of one thing, which cannot be written until a phrase can name a part. This plan makes that command writable; it does not build the change ([Not in scope](#not-in-scope)).

## What exists (read from code, 2026-10-23)

- **Two routes build a skeleton; attachment is not done on either.** The deterministic fast path (`maybeDeterministicManipulationIntent` in [`deterministicChecks.ts`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/discriminateIntent/deterministicChecks.ts)) claims every `take`/`drop`, and claims a `get` only when the whole tail is a room label. It makes the whole tail **one** span, so a qualifier is folded in; this is the shelf's `take` baseline. Everything else reaches the Nova Micro Parse ([`parse/buildParsePrompt.ts`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/enrich/objectManipulation/parse/buildParsePrompt.ts)), which emits a flat skeleton and, as probed 2026-10-23, **always splits** a qualifier into its own span ([corpus](AGENT.qualifiedReferents.corpus.planning.md), O1).
- **Plan groups a qualifier and lowers it** (Slice 2). `matchPlanTemplates` ([`plan/planSkeleton.ts`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/enrich/objectManipulation/plan/planSkeleton.ts)) runs the templates on each reading from `groupSkeleton` ([`plan/groupSkeleton.ts`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/enrich/objectManipulation/plan/groupSkeleton.ts): the closed lexicon `QUALIFIER_PREPOSITIONS`, and `take`/`get` + one closed-set tail folded into the head span's `qualifier`). It then puts a `containedBy` action ahead of each matched attempt's actions for every qualified span it names (`lowerQualifiers`, [`plan/lowerQualifiers.ts`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/enrich/objectManipulation/plan/lowerQualifiers.ts)). The membership template still reads only its first span. `compileAttemptsFromSkeleton` and Identify still get the flat skeleton, whose spans keep their keys.
- **The BD-20 gate refuses an unread span.** `compileAttemptsFromSkeleton` refuses a membership attempt as `complexErrorMessage('multiObject')` when the flat skeleton has a span the attempt does not name. A grouped take names both spans, through its assertion. A drop's tail, a tail outside the set, and a second qualifier are each left unread, so they are still refused.
- **Placement names direct hosts only and no relation kind; the cache holds the relation.** A thing's placement names the bindings that hold it directly (a pebble in a box names the box, not the room), and a binding's `under` is its parent, not spatial "under". The room cache's edges carry `On`/`In`, but the catalog handle exposes no edges (P6 clause 3). See corpus F1 and F2.
- **An assertion step is judged in the dry run** (Slice 1), **and Plan's lowering emits it** (Slice 2). `PlanStep`'s `Assertion` arm (`ContainedByAssertion { subject, object, negate, relation? }`, BD-14; [`plan/planStep.ts`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/enrich/objectManipulation/plan/planStep.ts)) is grounded with its referents kept (`groundPlanStep`), described ("Check: cup on table") by `proposeAttemptCandidates`, and judged by `attemptDryRun` (`containedByFailure`): every host of the subject's view must be the object, directly and whatever the relation, and a failure is illegal with "The table has no bag." (`qualifierErrorMessages`). `actions/AGENT.concepts.md` defines `containedBy` as disjunctive over membership/`On`, with three answers (`satisfied`, `violated`, `defer`). Commit skips it, and the executor has no lowering for it (shelved).
- **The dry run is where a precondition is judged.** `attemptDryRun` ([`attemptCandidates.ts`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/enrich/objectManipulation/attemptCandidates.ts)) grounds each action's step in order and checks per-primitive preconditions before seeding the executor. `extend` is judged there and not seeded, which is the shape an assertion needs. Its environment (`buildAttemptEnvironment`) holds every candidate's host graphs (with their `On`/`In` edges), its ancestry, and view hosts from placement. When every candidate is illegal, `selectPlanTuple` reports the highest-confidence candidate's reason. `commitAttempt` skips any action whose step is not a `change`.
- **Where things sit is already at the pool site.** Each catalog entry carries `placement`, folded from the room's ludicCache; [`objectViews.ts`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/objectViews.ts) reads it (`readingsOf`, `readingsHere`, `defaultReading`, `placementHostsOfView`). `proposeAttemptCandidates`' `viewChoicesFor` turns it into the readings a referent can mean, stamps a unique one, and asks among several. A qualifier needs no new world read.

## What this replaces

The earlier rung-6 plan (BD-24, named 2026-07-19, never started) was written before `placement` and `view` existed, and before any command was blocked on it. Its gate ("do not begin until a concrete case demands it") is met by the shelf bullets above.

- **Shelved with stacked phrases:** templates compose an object phrase once (`ObjectPhrase = OBJECTSPAN (PREP ObjectPhrase)?`); the user's replacement of BD-24's one-level, nearest-preceding attachment by full enumeration of bracketings (QR-2) went to the shelf with it.
- **Replaced (QR-1):** "the verb decides whether an attached phrase is an argument or a modifier." The question is smaller: does the phrase restrict state the system already knows, or fill a destination?
- **Kept, reversed 2026-10-23 (user):** a qualifier is a `ContainedByAssertion` (BD-14's remaining gap). An earlier draft dropped it on the grounds that an assertion differs only by checking when the step runs. That missed its **primary** value: the assertion is judged in the sandbox **dry run**, which removes the candidates it rules out and so disambiguates, through the selection loop every command already uses. The commit-time check is **secondary**, and is shelved (QR-4).

## Not in scope

- **Stacked qualifiers and `put`** ("put cup on saucer on table", "put the cup on the table in the box", "take cup on saucer on table", `look at the cup on the table`): bracketings, the depth cap, asking between readings with the same objects, and whether the fast path learns `put`. See "Shelved: stacked qualifiers and `put`" in [`actions/AGENT.implementation.md`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/AGENT.implementation.md).
- **A drop's tail as a destination** ("drop the cup in the box"): QR-7, on the same shelf.
- **Two parts of one object as two referents** ("tie the rope end on the table to the rope end in the room"): comparing (id, view) rather than id in `proposeAttemptCandidates`. This plan makes the command writable; the change waits for the user to want it.
- **Named parts** ("the end of the rope", "the part of the cloth over the table"): see "Shelved: wholes with named parts" in [`positions/AGENT.implementation.md`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/AGENT.implementation.md).
- **Spatial language the bindings do not hold** ("behind the door", "the second table", "next to the lamp").
- **Characters as qualifiers** ("the cup in Tess's hands"): the catalog walks only objects (the CPG-5 remainder).
- **Open phrasing.** Prepositions stay a closed set; a tail outside it defers to the LLM route.
- **Checking a qualifier at commit** (the assertion's secondary value; it matters most for compound commands): `commitAttempt` skips non-`change` steps, and the executor has no lowering for an assertion. Shelved under "Shelved: compound commands and cooperative actions".

| **QR-1** | **What does a phrase attached to an object mean?** *Decided 2026-10-23 (user):* a phrase that describes where something is now ("on the table", "from the table") is a **qualifier**: it restricts which object, and which of its bindings (`view`), a referent means, read from `placement`. A phrase that names where something goes ("put cup on table") is an **argument**, the destination. The preposition is a hint, not a role: "from" can only restrict, "on" and "in" can do either. The verb does not decide; "get bag from table" and "get the bag on the table" are the same filter. | Slices 1--3 | Decided |
| **QR-3** | **How does each route split a qualifier into its own span?** *LLM route, probed 2026-10-23:* it always does, chains included (corpus O1), so the prompt needs no change for it. Its article handling (O2) and `out of` (O3) are noise the closed preposition set must absorb. *Fast path, decided 2026-10-23 (user):* when the whole tail is an exact room label, keep it whole (today's behavior, which protects a name like "man in the moon"). Otherwise split at the closed-set preposition, and leave the pieces to Identify, which already matches misspellings. A `get` is claimed when its head piece (the words before the preposition) is a room label, where today the whole tail must be one. Labels guard the split; they are not required of every piece. | Slice 3 | Decided |
| **QR-4** | **How is a qualifier represented and judged?** *Decided 2026-10-23 (user), reworked after the user's correction (corpus [Requirements](AGENT.qualifiedReferents.corpus.planning.md#requirements-proposals-for-the-user-to-decide)):* (1) **An assertion, not a referent field.** It is an action whose step is `containedBy(subject, qualifyingSpan)`, placed before the take in the same attempt and covered by no narration unit. Referents and attempts do not change shape. The grouping that says which span a qualifier belongs to lives in the **skeleton** (`ObjectSpanToken.qualifier`, QR-8) and is lowered to this assertion when the attempt is compiled; it never reaches Identify or the candidate pool. (2) **The relation is recorded.** `ContainedByAssertion` gains an optional `relation` (`On` or `In`; absent means "held there, how unspecified"), set by the closed lexicon: `on`, `on top of`, `off` → `On`; `in`, `inside`, `out of` (LLM `out`) → `In`; `from` → absent. This grows the predicate per BD-14's discipline. (3) **How strictly it is judged is a separate, local choice:** for this plan, any direct host satisfies it, whatever the relation. The dry run holds the host graphs, so checking the `On`/`In` edge later needs no handle widening. **Lenient for now, by the user's call:** the user has ideas about `On`/`In` edges and will return to strict judging separately; what an edge-less hosting means is part of that. (4) **A view must sit wholly under the qualifier:** every binding of the subject's view, so the whole cloth fails "on the table" and its table part passes. (5) **Direct now; transitive later** is again evaluation only (the environment already holds each candidate's ancestry). (6) **The closed set** and its matching as before: multi-word first, word boundaries, never at a tail's head. | Slice 1 | Decided |
| **QR-8** | **Where does a qualifier attach to its span, so the next iteration can offer other attachments?** *Decided 2026-10-23 (user):* in one grouping step between the flat skeleton and the templates, and nowhere else. Both routes emit the flat skeleton (the fast path from Slice 3), so neither route decides attachment. The step returns a **list of readings**: a qualifier on the span ("modifies the referent") is the grouped form; a phrase left flat ("modifies the verb", today's `put bag in box`) is the flat form, so both readings already have a representation. This plan produces exactly one reading per command, chosen by a small verb table (`take`/`get` group; `drop` and every other verb stay flat). That table stands in for QR-1's "the verb does not decide" only until the next iteration emits several readings and lets the dry run and selection choose (the shelf's "Every bracketing is a reading"). The lowering to an assertion (QR-4) reads only the qualifier, not which reading produced it. | Slices 2--3 | Decided |
| **QR-6** | **What does a qualifier that matches nothing do?** *Decided 2026-10-23 (user):* refuse, naming the host and subject without claiming a relation: "The table has no bag." This applies when the subject and the qualifier each resolve, but every candidate fails the qualifier's assertion in the dry run. A qualifier is never dropped to rescue a take. A qualifier span that finds no match is today's unknown-span outcome. | Slice 2 | Decided |
| **QR-7** | **What does a drop's tail mean?** *Decided 2026-10-23 (user):* nothing, in this plan. A drop's tail is neither split nor read, so a drop never means anything but "drop it in the room": the fast path keeps folding the tail into one span, and the LLM route keeps refusing it as `multiObject`. Reading the tail as a destination is shelved. | Slices 1--3 | Decided |
