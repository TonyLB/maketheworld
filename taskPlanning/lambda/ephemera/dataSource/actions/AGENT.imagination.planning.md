# Imagination: the generating family's asks, read by hand

**Status (2026-11-12): steps 1, 1.5 and 2 done; this plan waits on the judging pieces (IM-4, user, 2026-11-12).** Opened from the [oracle loop plan](AGENT.oracleLoop.planning.md)'s Slice 4.5, when the play-by-play plan took its exit (c) ([trail](AGENT.oracleLoop.discussion.planning.md#the-imagination-answers-the-oracles-asks-the-play-by-plays-exit-c-2026-11-11)). That plan's drafting loop found that the play-by-play is one ask of a loop, not a stage: the orchestration layer asks, and the imagination answers one focused ask at a time. "More loops of a process we can _create_ are better than fewer loops of a process that will elude us" (user, 2026-11-11). Step 1 split the probe's folder by family and the drafts into a shared core and an ask part per ask. Step 1.5 sorted draft 10's composite text by ask and ended in a baseline of each (exit (d), user: both asks tune forward from the split). Step 2 brought `playByPlay` to draft 14, with every method at step 1.5's bar and no attempts ([batch 15](oracleLoopDrafts/playByPlay/14.notes.md#results-2026-11-12-batch-15)), and stopped at its checkpoint: the problems list does the job it was made for, holding the worries off the method (user, 2026-11-12), and how sensitive it should be, and the block's shape, are for the pieces that read them to say. **Next, in the parent:** the rule cell adjusted to the split asks, then the non-rule blocker and the other judging pieces. This plan resumes when a judging piece's reactions give the flags and the form something to tune against; step 3 then starts with the cup gap.

The imagination is the **family of generating prompts**, beside the oracle, the family of judging prompts (user, 2026-11-11). What the oracle loop plan called the improvisation engine is one of its asks: "propose a filler for this slot". This plan builds the asks one at a time on the piece probe's reading mode, starting with "more detail" from the play-by-play's draft 10. Composing the asks with the oracle's pieces is the composition probe's work, not this plan's.

Task plan framework: [`taskPlanning/AGENT.md`](../../../../AGENT.md), as an implementation plan under the parent's pilot of the [proposed design variant](../../../../AGENT.designVariant.proposed.md). The parent holds the subsystem map, the stages (a guess), the seam ledger and the corpus. This plan holds one episode. What it shows about a guess goes **up** to the parent's seam ledger.
