## EA-2: what must a whole-scale edge be able to say that the part-scale edges cannot?

**The two passes preserved below are the file's first worked example of the failure the [performance tally](../../../../AGENT.designVariant.performance.md) counts** --- the second pass went straight past [the inherited-settled row](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#what-is-inherited-as-settled-and-must-not-be-re-argued-here) forbidding whole and part to be typed, hours after that row was added for the same reason on EA-1. **The framing claim:** a whole-scale relation is an effort to let a relationship be **authored, recognized, or improvised at a different level of scale while reflecting the same fictional truth** --- exactly what a node abstraction is, one level up. **From which the row's stated question answers *nothing*.** If the coarse relation reflects the same truth, content it asserts *beyond* the parts is not abstraction but **new content minted under an abstraction's name**, and the node side already rejects that shape --- a `GrapplingHookGun` asserting a fact its parts contradict is not a coarse view of them. **This dissolves the apparent collision with [EA-4](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#ea-4)'s complementary-facts bar rather than losing to it: same truth, different facts.** Each scale is authoritative for the facts stated *at* its scale and neither restates the other. **The sharp corollary, which is a disposal rule this row can apply immediately:** any fact the coarse relation holds
