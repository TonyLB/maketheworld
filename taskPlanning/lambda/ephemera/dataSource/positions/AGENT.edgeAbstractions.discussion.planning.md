## EA-2: what must a whole-scale edge be able to say that the part-scale edges cannot?

**Graduated 2026-08-25**, closed as *malformed* rather than answered. The verdict is in the [Settled register](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#settled-register); what follows is the layered derivation the row accumulated over two prior passes, both of which reached the right answer by the wrong route.

**Recorded because it is an exception and must not read as precedent.** The plan's standard is that nothing graduates untested against a corpus case, and this row graduated without one --- **decided as a fresh choice on 2026-08-25, deliberately not written into the plan's instructions as a general allowance for reasoning-based verdicts**, on the ground that such an allowance is a loophole in waiting. **Any future row proposing to graduate this way owes the same fresh argument; this one is not a citable precedent for it.**

**The two passes preserved below are the file's first worked example of the failure the [performance tally](../../../../AGENT.designVariant.performance.md) counts** --- the second pass went straight past [the inherited-settled row](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#what-is-inherited-as-settled-and-must-not-be-re-argued-here) forbidding whole and part to be typed, hours after that row was added for the same reason on EA-1. The clause-by-clause hunt for a home is the shape that error takes when it is not caught.

**The framing claim:** a whole-scale relation is an effort to let a relationship be **authored, recognized, or improvised at a different level of scale while reflecting the same fictional truth** --- exactly what a node abstraction is, one level up. **From which the row's stated question answers *nothing*.** If the coarse relation reflects the same truth, content it asserts *beyond* the parts is not abstraction but **new content minted under an abstraction's name**, and the node side already rejects that shape --- a `GrapplingHookGun` asserting a fact its parts contradict is not a coarse view of them. **This dissolves the apparent collision with [EA-4](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only)'s complementary-facts bar rather than losing to it: same truth, different facts.** Each scale is authoritative for the facts stated *at* its scale and neither restates the other. **The sharp corollary, which is a disposal rule this row can apply immediately:** any fact the coarse relation holds that is **derivable by joining the parts is not a fact it holds at all** --- it is a cache of theirs, and belongs in [ludicCache](AGENT.abstractionLayers.ludicCache.corpus.planning.md) rather than on the relation. **The worked case is Ariadne's thread, and it reproduces [EA-4](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only)'s *expands into* resolution independently rather than by citing it:** `Skein -> Length of thread -> Tied end` is easier to reason with than the room-by-room chain **without foreclosing** the step-by-step encoding --- which for Ariadne will be built anyway, since *that ordering is information bearing from the moment of construction*. **A coarse relation whose fine scale is never dispensable cannot be a replacement**, which is the case EA-4 needed and did not have. ~~**What survives is a *where*, not a *what*, and it is deliberately not settled here.**~~ **Withdrawn by the third pass, whose verdict is in the [Settled register](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#settled-register) --- nothing survives, and the *where* was an artifact of the typing: a question about where coarse-only content lives presumes there is some.** Under [EA-7](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only)'s (a) the whole-scale relation is realized as **two edges and a node**, not as the single whole-scale edge this row's question presumes, so each candidate must be re-asked per home. **Retained as illustrations of the closed result rather than as open state --- each shows the disposal landing in a case the fiction actually reaches for, and none of them is the argument:** **(1) direction looks dissolved rather than answered** --- it is carried structurally by which flanking edge attaches to which terminal, so the `Skein` end and the `Tied end` end are distinguishable with no manner field at all; **(2) aperture is *licensing*, and on that reading it is not a fact this relation holds at all --- amended 2026-08-24 (second pass), which replaces the clause that stood here** (*aperture and reversibility survive, and they look like properties of the Arrangement node rather than of either flanking edge*). [PQ-8](AGENT.abstractionLayers.proposals.planning.md#open-questions-ab-34-sub-questions-ids-stable-never-reused) consequence (i), recorded 2026-08-20, already states that **AB-26's licensing label *is* a manner carrier** --- *"an aperture, a direction, a reversibility"* --- and that AB-26's own correction had already widened licensing **past Exit to any spatial relation of any provenance**, which is *"the widening AB-57's aperture needs."* **This row cites PQ-8 in its cross-references and narrowed without using it**, which is why the first pass went hunting for a home among node and flanking edges. **So aperture dissolves the way direction does in clause (1): into structure that already exists, not onto a new home.** Direction goes to which flanking edge attaches to which terminal; aperture goes to the **licensing relation**, which is a fact of the **boundary** --- authored once, shared by everything that crosses it, narrow for all comers --- and therefore neither a property of the Arrangement node nor of either flanking edge. **The presence port is likewise the wrong axis**, being per-object-per-host (the crosser's side) where licensing is per-boundary. **The `fromHostId` hedge is withdrawn:** it was contingent on the Arrangement-node home, and PQ-8 records the licensing label as **exterior world truth**, which is the same shape --- so the precedent below is apt rather than mis-aimed, though it now bears on the licensing relation rather than on this one. **The discipline PQ-8 attaches travels with the answer:** answering PQ-8's *where does the label live* alongside this row is sensible, **merging them is not** --- PQ-8 is a **part-to-part** edge within one whole, [AB-57](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only) asks whether the **host** carries anything, and this row asks about the **whole-scale relation**; three positions on one substrate, and collapsing them is the instance-taken-for-the-category error AB-26 was itself a correction of. **(3) reversibility needs no separate disposal, and the attempt to give it one produced the error most worth recording here.** The gloss *pull the cord back out* was argued in conversation from a ship built inside a bottle --- **which crosses nothing.** Its pieces crossed; the ship was **minted on the far side**, so *can it come back out* is a plain aperture question about an object with **no crossing history**, and node abstraction already disposes of it with no manner field anywhere. **The argument had read a change of *referent* as a change of *state*, which is the same typing error one level down** --- treating "the crosser" as a durable kind that persists across an assembly. **The examples that survive that objection do not need history either:** a nail driven into wood and a rope threaded through a ring and then knotted both keep identity across the crossing, and in both the irreversibility is readable off the **current** configuration against the opening --- so it is aperture evaluated on the object as it now stands, and therefore licensing again. **What this bears on elsewhere, to be recorded there rather than claimed here:** aperture and reversibility were the two candidates named for [EA-1](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only) clause (vii)'s owed overlap case --- a coarse fact **not** derivable by joining parts --- and the closure recorded in the [Settled register](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#settled-register) says no such fact can exist. **That reads as discharging the debt rather than defaulting on it, but the discharge is EA-1's to record, not this row's.** **The precedent this row hands [EA-9](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only) has moved with the framing claim** --- see there; the move rides on the *authored* leg of the tri-modal and is likewise a lean rather than a finding.
