## Why this is a separate plan, and why it is opened now

**The trigger, from conversation 2026-08-21: presence is the missing articulation that [P7 --- merge-reduce](AGENT.abstractionLayers.ludicCache.corpus.planning.md#mechanism-findings-staged-for-p7) needs before it can be authored.**

The staged findings state the reducer's unit as *"a merge resolves a set of terminals; each resolved terminal is queried against the cache and against the retained graphs; each hit is composed or walked onward"* --- and **every clause of that is a presence operation.** *Queried against the retained graphs* is a presence predicate. Finding 4's sub-graphing **drops** things, and what may be dropped is a presence judgement. Finding 5's recovery is *"a keyed walk, not a scan"*, and the key is a presence key. The parent's [flag on CC1b](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#recommended-order) put this in the negative form already: under the reducer, **node presence and edge composition are the same operation** --- which is precisely why the reducer cannot be specified while presence is a word rather than a system.

**Writing P7 first would decide presence by implication**, in the vocabulary of a reducer, in whichever form made that reducer easy to write. That is the failure mode this initiative has a name for.

**The second reason is the parent's own methodological one**, and it applies here more sharply than anywhere it has been applied yet:

> *What must the model be able to express?* is answerable from the fiction with confidence. *What is the representation?* is not, and depends on the first. **A row asking both makes the answerable half unreachable.**

**[AB-8](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only) has been open since 2026-08-05 and has been re-framed at least four times** --- through H1's removal of multi-hosting, through P3's single-use ports, through the port-list carrier, through C10's apprehension-scale qualifier. **It is a representation row that has never had a requirement half.** This plan exists to supply it.

## Two things are called presence, and whether they are one thing is PR-2

**Stated first because the ambiguity is live in the corpus, in the durable docs, and in the shipped code at once**, and a reader who does not have it will merge two systems by accident.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Where it is stated | [`AGENT.concepts.md`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/AGENT.concepts.md) --- *"At play time, which room a character occupies"* | [AB-8](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only)'s (a)-with-ports re-frame, 2026-08-09 |

**They are not obviously the same and not obviously different.** Under [locked-frame clause 3](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#locked-frame-parts-and-ports-2026-08-06) every host has a graph of the same internal structure whatever kind it is, so *a character in a room* and *a rope in a room* are both **a node in a room's graph** --- which argues one relation. But the shipped machinery for the first is entirely separate from the mechanism proposed for the second, one is single-valued and terminally enforced while the other exists to be multi-valued, and their write rates differ by orders of magnitude.

**Do not resolve this in prose.** It is [PR-2](#open-decisions-design--plan-only), it is a **requirement** question before it is a representation one, and it is probably the row that decides the shape of everything else here.

**One thing has already shrunk it, 2026-08-21.** [PR-10](#open-decisions-design--plan-only) was answered *yes* --- presence is left implicit wherever a thing can only be in one place --- the right-hand column of this table is now the only one with a representation question in it: characters, being single-valued and terminally gated, carry no presence record to compare against. **This does not answer PR-2** --- the two might still be one relation or two in the fiction --- but the answer now costs nothing in storage, which is a materially smaller row to argue than the one this section was written for.

### The one inherited clause that most changes how this plan should be read

**Presence is truth.** The sibling sub-plan on attention is licensed to be lossy, sampled, heuristically pruned and aggressively compacted, because *a miss falls through* and the worst outcome is slow. **None of that license transfers here.** Presence answers *where is it*, and a wrong answer is a wrong world --- an object in a room it is not in, or absent from one it is in. Designs that would be perfectly reasonable for the attention ledger are **disqualified** in this plan, and the argument for them will sound familiar because it was accepted one construct over.

## Presence as a cover

**Working formulation, from conversation 2026-08-21. Not settled** --- nothing is in the [Settled register](#settled-register) and this section is not a graduated row. It is written down because the open rows below are phrased in its vocabulary and are unreadable without it.

**Presence is a cover of a whole's graph, indexed by the hosts the whole is present in.** For each host, presence names a subset of the whole's nodes: the part of the whole that is *there*. Buckets **may overlap**. Their union **must be every node** --- that totality condition is [PR-9](#open-decisions-design--plan-only).

Three levels, and keeping them apart is what makes the rows below separable:

| Level | What it fixes | Row |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The whole** | An abstraction-fractal level. Has its own graph and a root, per locked-frame clause 1 | Parent plan, settled |
| **A node in that graph** | A **part** at this level --- one of the elements the whole is constructed from in aggregate | Parent plan, settled |
| **The cover** | How that breakdown plays out **relative to the hosting of the whole**: which parts are present in which host | **This plan** |

**The candidate mechanism** is a **walk over presence-bearing relational edges**: each bucket is the reachability walk from a host's presence port, following only edges whose kind carries presence. The aggregation of the buckets from every host the whole is present in reconstitutes the whole graph. Under this mechanism, PR-9's totality stops being a lean and becomes **a checkable invariant** --- every node reachable from at least one presence port over presence-bearing edges.

**This is a new *kind* on the existing relational edge, not a parallel edge layer.** `ludicGraph` already carries uuid-keyed, typed, **directed** `{ from, to, kind }` relational edges through one shared serde across every host kind, with a WML convergence path staked out for the wire types. A separate structure walked independently would duplicate all of that. **Which kind or kinds the walk follows is [PR-4](#open-decisions-design--plan-only), answered provisionally 2026-08-21 as reading (d): a `Present` kind, and the walk is distinguished from composition and peer edges by *where the edge starts* --- port -> node --- rather than by a hierarchy among kinds.** Read PR-4 before treating that as settled; it is not graduated, and it carries a dependency on shipped-code work.

**Two derivations have already failed, and both failed the same way.** [C10](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c10-the-moonbase-computer-system) falsified *at least one port egressing here*; plain graph connectivity falsifies *reachable from the port*, because a connected whole makes every bucket the entire graph and the cover says the rope is entirely in both rooms at once. **Both tried to read presence off structure drawn for other purposes.** A dedicated edge type is declared structure whose only job is to carry this --- which is why [PR-4](#open-decisions-design--plan-only) is no longer a derived-versus-declared question.

**What this formulation buys, recorded so the rows are not re-argued from scratch:** [PR-1](#open-decisions-design--plan-only)'s hardest demand --- *where along a spanning thing an attachment sits* --- is answered structurally, as *which bucket*. [PR-10](#open-decisions-design--plan-only) gets a reason rather than a lean: single-**presence**-ported means a **one-bucket cover**, which is total by construction and identical to membership. (*Single-hosted* until [PR-C1](AGENT.presence.corpus.planning.md#pr-c1-the-contraption-in-two-rooms-and-whether-covers-nest); a whole may hold two ports from one host, so **port count is the predicate and it is inherited from the parent, not read off the thing**.) [PR-2](#open-decisions-design--plan-only)'s third answer gets its argument: character presence and extent become **one relation at different cardinality**, one bucket versus many. And [PR-8](#open-decisions-design--plan-only) gets its first candidate sentence, because a merge-reduce **sub-graph is a bucket**, what sub-graphing *drops* is everything outside it, and *walked onward* is crossing into an adjacent bucket through the port.
