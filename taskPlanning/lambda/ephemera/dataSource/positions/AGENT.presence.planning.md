## Why this is a separate plan, and why it is opened now

**The trigger, from conversation 2026-08-21: presence is the missing articulation that [merge-reduce](AGENT.ludicCacheRebuild.planning.md#the-mechanism-relocated-from-the-corpus) --- the once-reserved `P7` slot, superseded by [`AGENT.ludicCacheRebuild.planning.md`](AGENT.ludicCacheRebuild.planning.md) needs before it can be authored.**

The staged findings state the reducer's unit as *"a merge resolves a set of terminals; each resolved terminal is queried against the cache and against the retained graphs; each hit is composed or walked onward"* --- and **every clause of that is a presence operation.** *Queried against the retained graphs* is a presence predicate. Finding 4's sub-graphing **drops** things, and what may be dropped is a presence judgement. Finding 5's recovery is *"a keyed walk, not a scan"*, and the key is a presence key. The parent's [flag on CC1b](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#recommended-order) put this in the negative form already: under the reducer, **node presence and edge composition are the same operation** --- which is precisely why the reducer cannot be specified while presence is a word rather than a system.

**Writing the reducer first (`P7`, as the slot was then called) would decide presence by implication**, in the vocabulary of a reducer, in whichever form made that reducer easy to write. That is the failure mode this initiative has a name for.

**The second reason is the parent's own methodological one**, and it applies here more sharply than anywhere it has been applied yet:

> *What must the model be able to express?* is answerable from the fiction with confidence. *What is the representation?* is not, and depends on the first. **A row asking both makes the answerable half unreachable.**

**[AB-8](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only) has been open since 2026-08-05 and has been re-framed at least four times** --- through H1's removal of multi-hosting, through P3's single-use ports, through the port-list carrier, through C10's apprehension-scale qualifier. **It is a representation row that has never had a requirement half.** This plan exists to supply it.

## Two things are called presence, and whether they are one thing is PR-2

**Stated first because the ambiguity is live in the corpus, in the durable docs, and in the shipped code at once**, and a reader who does not have it will merge two systems by accident.

**They are not obviously the same and not obviously different.** Under [locked-frame clause 3](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#locked-frame-parts-and-ports-2026-08-06) every host has a graph of the same internal structure whatever kind it is, so *a character in a room* and *a rope in a room* are both **a node in a room's graph** --- which argues one relation. But the shipped machinery for the first is entirely separate from the mechanism proposed for the second, one is single-valued and terminally enforced while the other exists to be multi-valued, and their write rates differ by orders of magnitude.

**Do not resolve this in prose.** It is [PR-2](#settled-register), it is a **requirement** question before it is a representation one, and it is probably the row that decides the shape of everything else here.

**One thing has already shrunk it, 2026-08-21.** [PR-10](#settled-register) was answered *yes* --- presence is left implicit wherever a thing can only be in one place --- the right-hand column of this table is now the only one with a representation question in it: characters, being single-valued and terminally gated, carry no presence record to compare against. **This does not answer PR-2** --- the two might still be one relation or two in the fiction --- but the answer now costs nothing in storage, which is a materially smaller row to argue than the one this section was written for.

**[PR-2](#settled-register) was then answered 2026-08-21: one relation, with character hosting restricted to `ROOM`.** **This section is kept rather than rewritten, because the ambiguity it warns about is still live in the durable docs and the shipped code**, and a reader still needs the two columns to see what was merged. **Read it now as the statement of a resolved question, not an open one** --- and note that the resolution was a **scoping decision** about how much complexity the character path should carry today, not a discovery about the fiction. The row states what lifts it.

### The one inherited clause that most changes how this plan should be read

**Presence is truth.** The sibling sub-plan on attention is licensed to be lossy, sampled, heuristically pruned and aggressively compacted, because *a miss falls through* and the worst outcome is slow. **None of that license transfers here.** Presence answers *where is it*, and a wrong answer is a wrong world --- an object in a room it is not in, or absent from one it is in. Designs that would be perfectly reasonable for the attention ledger are **disqualified** in this plan, and the argument for them will sound familiar because it was accepted one construct over.

## Presence as a cover

~~**Working formulation, from conversation 2026-08-21. Not settled** --- nothing is in the [Settled register](#settled-register) and this section is not a graduated row.~~ **Corrected 2026-08-21 later the same day: the second clause went stale within hours and it matters, because rows are told to treat this section as freely revisable.** **This section is still not itself a graduated row** --- it has no ID and no verdict cell --- **but two rows phrased in its vocabulary have now graduated**, and they pin parts of it: [PR-9](#settled-register) settles **totality** (over nodes, over hosted wholes) and [PR-10](#settled-register) settles the **at-most-one-binding** predicate, which is phrased in the index this section defines. **So the revisable surface has shrunk to what those two verdicts do not touch.** Changing what the cover is **indexed by**, or what its **domain** is, now contradicts a settled row rather than merely amending a working note --- and would have to go back through the register. It is written down because the open rows below are phrased in its vocabulary and are unreadable without it.

**Nothing in this formulation is ever serialised, hoisted here 2026-08-21 from conversation because it was stated only in [PR-1](#settled-register)'s method note and a reader meets the vocabulary first.** **There is no `buckets` field and no `cover` record.** Stored: nodes, edges, presence bindings. **Derived: buckets and the cover** --- and for an at-most-one-binding whole, not even derived, since the single bucket **is** the node list. **A stored bucket set would be a second encoding of what edges and bindings already determine, so it could disagree with them, with no tiebreak available because the derived answer is the definition.** That is the same argument that makes presence implicit under [PR-10](#settled-register). **The cover is what the storage is graded against, never a copy of it.**

**Two senses of *present*, disambiguated 2026-08-21 from conversation, and this must be read first because the formulation below uses the word in only one of them.** **Membership --- *is the whole there at all* --- is DERIVED**, from where its parts are: [C10](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c10-the-moonbase-computer-system)'s port into the lab exists **because** `Terminal1` is there. **Apprehensibility --- *does the whole answer "what is here" at that host's scale* --- is DECLARED**, and cannot be derived: rope and moonbase computer have identical presence-port topology and need different room-scale answers, which is C10's finding and is why **apprehension scale** is a declared field. **Everything below is the membership sense.** **The two were fused in C10's own opening wording and briefly re-fused on 2026-08-21** while [PH0d](#recommended-order) was closing --- the closure argument (*the index is minted from where the parts are*) is a membership claim, and C10's *"that is the wrong answer"* is an apprehensibility claim, **so they never actually disagreed.** **Do not let this collapse back into one rule; the corpus case that looks like a counter-example to derived membership is [annotated at the source](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c10-the-moonbase-computer-system) as not being one.**

~~**Presence is a cover of a whole's graph, indexed by the hosts the whole is present in.** For each host, presence names a subset of the whole's nodes: the part of the whole that is *there*.~~ **Corrected 2026-08-21 from conversation: the index is *finer than the host set*, and this sentence had it backwards.** **Presence is a cover of a whole's graph, indexed by the whole's *presence bindings* --- the distinguishable ways it is present, mechanised today as presence ports.** For each binding, presence names a subset of the whole's nodes: the part of the whole that is *there*. **The host is a *property* of a bucket, not its key.** **[PR-C1](AGENT.presence.corpus.planning.md#pr-c1-the-contraption-in-two-rooms-and-whether-covers-nest) tabulates the counter-example to host-indexing and this formulation never absorbed it:** `GrapplingHookGun` has `port_1` and `port_2` with **both** `fromHostId: OBJECT#Contraption`, and buckets `{Gun, CableSpan1}` / `{CableSpan2, Hook}` that are **disjoint**. **Index those by host and they merge, destroying the split the case exists to demonstrate.** **Note how the error survived: PR-C1's amendment was applied to [PR-10](#settled-register)'s predicate --- *port count, not host count* --- and never to the formulation the predicate is phrased in**, so the corrected row and the uncorrected vocabulary sat two screens apart for a day, **and the stale half is the one that got transcribed into a durable doc.** **Why the index cannot be the host set, stated so it does not regress:** port arity is **inherited**, so the ways a whole is present are inherited too; the host set is not, and collapsing to it discards exactly the inherited distinctions [PR-10](#settled-register) is about. **What is preserved --- checked rather than assumed:** *the root is in every bucket* survives untouched, since it turns on the root **being** the whole and not on what keys the index, and [PR-C1](AGENT.presence.corpus.planning.md#pr-c1-the-contraption-in-two-rooms-and-whether-covers-nest) already says so directly (*"each root is in every bucket of its own graph by definition"*) --- its *disjoint* refers to the non-root members it lists. **Likewise the PH0d closure argument holds**: a bucket exists because the whole is present at that binding, so a bucket the whole is absent from is still unproducible. **The semantics/mechanism line is preserved by naming the index *presence binding* rather than *port*.** That the index is **finer than the host set** is semantics, and survives the walk being abandoned; that a binding is realised as a **port** is [PR-4](#settled-register)'s mechanism and stays swappable. Buckets **may overlap**. Their union **must be every node** --- that totality condition is [PR-9](#settled-register).

Three levels, and keeping them apart is what makes the rows below separable:

| Level | What it fixes | Row |
| --- | --- | --- |

| **A node in that graph** | A **part** at this level --- one of the elements the whole is constructed from in aggregate | Parent plan, settled |
| **The cover** | How that breakdown plays out **relative to the hosting of the whole**: which parts are present in which host | **This plan** |

**The candidate mechanism** is a **walk over presence-bearing relational edges**: each bucket is the reachability walk from a host's presence port, following only edges whose kind carries presence. ~~The aggregation of the buckets from every host the whole is present in reconstitutes the whole graph.~~ **Corrected 2026-08-21: it reconstitutes every *node* of the graph, which is all the cover ever claimed** --- the cover ranges over nodes only, so **edges are not recovered by aggregation and were never in a bucket to recover.** Edges come from the graph, which is what is stored. **The mis-reading this guards against is not benign:** a reader who takes *reconstitutes the graph* literally builds aggregation as a per-bucket edge union, **which silently drops every edge spanning two buckets** --- and those are required by this design, not tolerated by it ([C7](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c7-ariadnes-thread)'s cross-room span connections are what make its route recoverable). **So the aggregate of the buckets is the node list, and the graph is node list plus edges.** Under this mechanism, PR-9's totality stops being a lean and becomes **a checkable invariant** --- every node reachable from at least one presence port over presence-bearing edges.

**What that check is aimed at, sharpened 2026-08-21 and recorded here because this sentence is what other rows cite.** Totality is maintained **by construction** --- ports are minted from where the parts are --- so **the fiction cannot produce a violation**, and [PH0d](#recommended-order) closed on being unable to write one. **That is a theorem, not a gap.** What the check earns its keep against is an **incomplete constructor**: *by construction* here is a claim about a **constructor discipline** rather than about arithmetic, and [PR-10](#settled-register)'s Obligation A names the way it falls short --- a nested straddling whole whose top-level cover is well-formed while *"the interior ones are silently unaligned."* **So read *checkable* as verifying the write paths and the cascade, never as interrogating the world.**

**This is a new *kind* on the existing relational edge, not a parallel edge layer.** `ludicGraph` already carries uuid-keyed, typed, **directed** `{ from, to, kind }` relational edges through one shared serde across every host kind, with a WML convergence path staked out for the wire types. A separate structure walked independently would duplicate all of that. **Which kind or kinds the walk follows is [PR-4](#settled-register), answered provisionally 2026-08-21 as reading (d): a `Present` kind, and the walk is distinguished from composition and peer edges by *where the edge starts* --- port -> node --- rather than by a hierarchy among kinds.** Read PR-4 before treating that as settled; it is not graduated, and it carries a dependency on shipped-code work.

**Two derivations have already failed, and both failed the same way.** [C10](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c10-the-moonbase-computer-system) falsified *at least one port egressing here*; plain graph connectivity falsifies *reachable from the port*, because a connected whole makes every bucket the entire graph and the cover says the rope is entirely in both rooms at once. **Both tried to read presence off structure drawn for other purposes.** A dedicated edge type is declared structure whose only job is to carry this --- which is why [PR-4](#settled-register) is no longer a derived-versus-declared question.

**What this formulation buys, recorded so the rows are not re-argued from scratch:** [PR-1](#settled-register)'s hardest demand --- *where along a spanning thing an attachment sits* --- is answered structurally, as *which bucket*. [PR-10](#settled-register) gets a reason rather than a lean: single-**presence**-ported means a **one-bucket cover**, which is total by construction and identical to membership. (*Single-hosted* until [PR-C1](AGENT.presence.corpus.planning.md#pr-c1-the-contraption-in-two-rooms-and-whether-covers-nest); a whole may hold two ports from one host, so **port count is the predicate and it is inherited from the parent, not read off the thing**.) [PR-2](#settled-register)'s third answer gets its argument: character presence and extent become **one relation at different cardinality**, one bucket versus many. And [PR-8](#open-decisions-design--plan-only) gets its first candidate sentence, because a merge-reduce **sub-graph is a bucket**, what sub-graphing *drops* is everything outside it, and *walked onward* is crossing into an adjacent bucket through the port.
