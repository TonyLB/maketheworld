**Status: in progress. [D1](#open-decisions-implementation--plan-only) decided 2026-09-13: fold. Next step: [Slice 0](#recommended-order)'s remaining items --- decide D2, move the corpus's staged findings, and sweep inbound P7 pointers.**

## Tier, dependency tag, and rollback trigger

**Carried forward, not re-elected.** Iteration 1 of `ludicCache` was elected a **Prototype** on 2026-08-13 under the design variant's [graduation tiers](../../../../AGENT.designVariant.md#graduation-tiers-when-a-decision-licenses-code), and [`mergeReducer.ts`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/ludicCache/mergeReducer.ts)'s header names the tag and trigger for the code that exists. **A standard plan has no tiers of its own, so it inherits rather than invents one** --- restated here so the license is visible in the file a builder actually opens.

- **Dependency tag --- the rollback set, widened by this plan:** `positions/ludicGraph/presenceSubGraph.ts`, `positions/ludicCache/*`, their fixtures and tests, plus whatever Slices 3--6 add under `positions/ludicCache/`. **Consumers are not in the set**, which is what [D6](#open-decisions-implementation--plan-only) exists to keep true.
- **Rollback trigger, unchanged:** *a bucket cannot be stated from the child's own graph plus its ports* --- i.e. if deciding which nodes are in a binding turns out to require the parent's graph, presence is not port-indexed and the reducer's premise fails.
- **Second trigger, added by this plan:** [P6 clause 3](AGENT.abstractionLayers.proposals.planning.md#the-five-clauses) proving unholdable against the first real consumer (Slice 4) --- a hit must return a **handle**, never a subgraph. This is the one violation that propagates past the seam.
- **Not triggers:** rebuild latency, cache size, read counts, fixture verbosity. **Those are the measurements this Prototype exists to take**, and naming a cost as a trigger is how a prototype stops producing evidence and starts producing anxiety.

**When does it lock?** [D7](#open-decisions-implementation--plan-only). PH3's checkpoint on 2026-09-11 said *not yet*, explicitly; the honest answer is "when Slice 4 puts a consumer on it," and that is a decision to make out loud, not to drift into.

## Why this is not proposal P7

**P7 was a reserved, unwritten slot in the proposal record for the merge-reduce reducer, cited as a gate by CC1, PR-8 and PR-17.** This plan supersedes that gate rather than satisfying it. Three reasons, recorded because the next reader will find live pointers to P7 and should know they were retired deliberately.

1. **It fails the design variant's own admission test.** [`AGENT.designVariant.md`](../../../../AGENT.designVariant.md#when-you-are-in-this-variant) requires all three of: the deliverable is a decision not a diff; the questions outnumber the steps; the evidence has to be built as a corpus. The deliverable here is a rebuild function, a wiring change and an instrumentation pass. The base doc is explicit: *"If you are unsure, you are writing an implementation plan."*
2. **A proposal record holds what you are choosing between, and there is no rival scheme.** Of the six staged findings, 3, 4 and 5 were settled or narrowed in their own text; finding 1's feared primitive is moot (see [the mechanism](#the-mechanism-relocated-from-the-corpus)); finding 2 argues its own verdict. A proposal whose content is one already-argued position is a report filed in the class of file reserved for rivalry.
3. **The precedent is one month old and points here.** `AGENT.ludicCacheReducer.planning.md` was a standard implementation plan, deliberately not gated on any row, and it is what produced the reduction convention (LR-1/LR-6/LR-8) that closed PR-8's question and corrected CC0's edge shape. **The design answers came out of a build plan.**

**What P7-as-a-gate actually was: a prediction made on 2026-08-21, when ports did not exist in code and presence was a word rather than a system.** Both conditions have since changed. **The obligations hanging off it are real and are adopted here** --- [D1](#open-decisions-implementation--plan-only) (CC1b's flag), [D2](#open-decisions-implementation--plan-only) (P8 row 5), and Slice 0's relocation. **Skipping the proposal is not skipping those.**

## The mechanism, relocated from the corpus

**Moved here by Slice 0 from [the corpus's staged-findings section](AGENT.abstractionLayers.ludicCache.corpus.planning.md#mechanism-findings-staged-for-p7)**, whose own instruction was *"move them out when P7 lands, and delete this section then."* Substituting this plan for P7 keeps that instruction intact. Condensed to what a builder needs; the derivations stay in the corpus's case text.

1. **Compose at crossing ports, never at nodes.** A merge may collapse two edges sharing a **crossing-port** terminal; never two sharing a **node** terminal. Crossing ports are not referents and nodes are, so composing across a port destroys nothing and composing across a node destroys something a player could have named. **A possible/impossible rule, not a preference.**

   **Its "required primitive" is already shipped, and the hazard it warned of is moot.** The finding demanded a port-aware terminal discriminator, on PQ-9's finding that `isEphemeraObjectId` would *crash* on a `#`-separated port address. Ports are structured values now: `EphemeraLudicPortAddress` is `{ owner, port }`, `EphemeraLudicTerminalId` is a union, `isEphemeraLudicPortAddress` exists, and [`ephemeraMeta.ts`](../../../../../packages/mtw-interfaces/ts/ephemeraMeta.ts) states outright that *the parsed shape IS the stored shape --- no string form*. `mergeReducer.ts` already discriminates this way. **Do not re-import the string-parsing hazard.**
2. **General edge-merge contains edge-stitch, and the argument is not efficiency.** Stitch is merge over a chain of length two. The general form is arguably *less* code --- stitch carries a case split (append a crossing and rewrite the endpoint, versus prepend and rewrite the start) that composition does not, since direction falls out of which terminal is shared. **The narrow form is the same code with an unsettled premise baked in as a restriction.** This is the finding [D1](#open-decisions-implementation--plan-only) turns on.
3. **Entitlement and retention are two questions, and only the first is load-bearing.** *May the reducer consult a graph it has already merged?* --- **yes, settled**, forced by [LC8](AGENT.abstractionLayers.ludicCache.corpus.planning.md#lc8-the-spring-in-the-box-and-in-the-contraption)'s sub-graphing argument. *In memory, or shard ids re-read on demand?* --- same semantics, different cost: [D4](#open-decisions-implementation--plan-only).
4. **Do not drop anything during sub-graphing is rejected, recorded so it is not re-proposed.** It would make the cache self-describing and remove recovery entirely, and it fails for the reason the cache exists: **unbounded size is what the ragged frontier and `interiorConsolidated` are there to control.**
5. **Recovery is a keyed walk, not a scan.** Every terminal that becomes newly present is a trigger; look it up against retained graphs; follow the chain, possibly across several graphs, until it grounds. **Complete because an edge is recoverable only if at least one terminal is now present, and every such terminal is a trigger.** **No index and no new store is needed** --- the port record's egress list supplies the outward half and the interior graph's own edges supply the inward half. What remains is a **lookup discipline**: both halves must be consulted, and neither direction quietly implemented one-way. A one-way walk **silently returns nothing**, which is the failure mode to write a test against rather than to guard.
6. **Triggering on newly-present terminals collapses stitching and recovery into one mechanism.** Starting from *edges left dangling in the cache* under-covers, because an edge dropped while **both** terminals were ungrounded leaves no dangling trace. Triggering on **every terminal that becomes representable** covers it --- and extending a cache edge into a host that just consolidated and recovering an edge sub-graphing discarded become the same operation. **This is a simplification only if a terminal is looked up the same way in both stores**; if the cache is searched one way and retained graphs another, the two mechanisms are back wearing one name.

**What the shipped code already does with all of this.** [`collapseCrossingPorts`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/ludicCache/mergeReducer.ts) implements findings 1 and 2 for the **pairwise** case: it composes at crossing ports only, excludes presence ports, asserts both legs agree on identity fields, rewrites the port terminal to the real interior node, and groups by identity so two ports collapsing to the same `(from, to, kind)` land on one record with two `chains` entries side by side. [`subGraphFromNodes`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/ludicGraph/presenceSubGraph.ts) implements the cut, with stub ports minted transiently and never persisted. **Findings 3, 5 and 6 have no code at all** --- they are about the fold across many graphs, which is Slice 3.

Full note, including why `existencePresenceGuard` is not this check: [`enrich/objectManipulation/AGENT.md`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/enrich/objectManipulation/AGENT.md) step 0.

No influence on `T_MARGIN`, no attention-based pre-filtering. An exhaustive cache complies trivially, so this costs nothing now and is here to be inherited rather than rediscovered.
  - [ ] **Force a miss that the ordinary path cannot produce** (CC5). ~~Under an exhaustive cache nothing misses, so the fall-through is dead code that first runs on the day the cache stops being exhaustive.~~ **Amended by [D10](#open-decisions-implementation--plan-only): at this slice there is no fall-through to build or test** --- the cache is the sole pool source, and CC5's *build the dead path deliberately* obligation **moves to Slice 6**, where persistence first makes a miss possible. **What stays here, and is now the whole of the item: terminal semantics.** A port terminal means *not consolidated here*, and is positive evidence something further exists --- never *nothing further exists*. With nothing behind the cache that distinction is the only thing preventing a silent no-match, so test it directly rather than as documentation.
