## PR-1. Requirement: what must "X is present in H" be able to express?

**Graduated 2026-08-21.** Verdict and date live in the [Settled register](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register); this is the derivation, moved verbatim from the row rather than re-summarised.

**Row references:** [C7](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c7-ariadnes-thread), [C10](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c10-the-moonbase-computer-system); PH0

### Argument as the row carried it

**Requirement: what must "X is present in H" be able to express?** Enumerate before representing. Candidate demands already visible: multi-host extent (a thread in three rooms); **held-and-present** (`Theseus -Held-> thread:1` and `thread:2 -In-> room` over one thread graph); *where along* a spanning thing an attachment sits; and whether presence is graded --- present-and-apprehensible versus present-but-only-by-traversal. **Do not answer with a data structure.** **Answered 2026-08-21 from conversation. Four candidate demands were listed; three are answered, and the fourth was two questions sharing a name and splits.** **Method note first, because this row's own instruction is what nearly got broken.** The [cover formulation](AGENT.presence.planning.md#presence-as-a-cover) was briefly treated here as the data structure this row forbids. **It is not one, and the test that settles it is *would you serialise it?*** --- and you would not. What is stored is edges, ports and membership rows; **the cover is what that storage is graded against**, which is why [PR-9](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register) already calls totality *a checkable invariant* and why all four of [PR-3](AGENT.presence.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only)'s rival carriers can be scored by it rather than one of them being it. **So the swap-out test for this row is not *would this survive the cover being abandoned* --- it is *would this survive the **walk** being abandoned*.** Buckets, ~~index-by-host~~ **index-by-presence-binding** (corrected 2026-08-21; see the [cover formulation](AGENT.presence.planning.md#presence-as-a-cover) --- the index is finer than the host set, and *finer than hosts* is the semantic claim while *realised as a port* is mechanism), totality and aggregation are this row's own vocabulary; ports, `Present` edges and reachability are [PR-4](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register)'s mechanism and must stay swappable. Each demand below is stated so that it passes that test. **(1) Extent without ranking, and presence not keyed to host kind.** The first two candidates merge. *Multi-host extent* is the requirement that a thing be present in several hosts **with none privileged as *the* location** --- not that it have a location which happens to be plural. *Held-and-present* then needs no separate device: `EphemeraMembershipHostId` is `Room \| Character \| Object \| Feature \| Area` ([`ephemeraPositionAdjacency.ts`](../../../../../packages/mtw-interfaces/ts/ephemeraPositionAdjacency.ts)), **one union of five kinds**, so `Theseus -Held-> thread:1` alongside `thread:2 -In-> room` is a two-host extent over a heterogeneous index set and nothing arbitrates between them. **[C7](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c7-ariadnes-thread)'s finding --- *held-and-present are not mutually exclusive and nothing can currently say both* --- therefore restates as a demand that presence not be keyed to host kind**, which the shipped union already satisfies. **The thing that would now need arguing is exclusivity**, and that inversion is the whole gain. **(2) Where along a spanning thing an attachment sits**, stated walk-free: presence must locate an attachment **relative to the extent** rather than only relative to the whole. Answered structurally as *which bucket* in the [cover formulation](AGENT.presence.planning.md#presence-as-a-cover); transcribed here as the requirement rather than re-argued. **(3) A whole is not necessarily present where its parts are.** This is the surviving half of the fourth candidate, and it comes from [C10](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c10-the-moonbase-computer-system). **The split, recorded as a correction because the original wording fused two questions under one name.** *Whether presence is graded --- present-and-apprehensible versus present-but-only-by-traversal* is two demands. **(a) Apprehension scale stays, and it is a truth question.** C10 defines the field as *"at which level is this thing the answer to **what is here**"* and argues it as an instance of **scale-relative truth** --- *"'There is a terminal here' is not a lossy version of 'there is a computer system here' --- it is correct at room scale, as 'there is a rope here' is correct at room scale."* It also does work **in this plan** rather than downstream: it is what **falsified [C9](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c9-coiling-the-rope-back-in)'s membership rule**, which had put the whole computer system in the lab on port topology alone. **(b) Grading by noticeability leaves.** *Present but only by traversal* grades how hard a part is to notice **given that it is already in the bucket** --- and if it is in the bucket it is in the host. Keeping it here would make presence answer *what is immediately apprehensible* instead of *what is true*. **Rehomed to the parent's apprehension-versus-description axis, and that homing was already ruled rather than newly chosen:** the [ludicCache corpus](AGENT.abstractionLayers.ludicCache.corpus.planning.md#the-corpus) sends [C15](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c15-the-microphone-the-wire-and-the-speaker-two-rooms-away) away in terms --- *"about apprehension and description and belongs to the parent's axis"* --- **so it is not a `ludicCache` question either.** [PR-4](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register)'s cost-on-a-`Present`-edge candidate is struck there on this reasoning. **The split pays because (3) is not a fourth demand at all --- it is the zero-host question wearing another name.** C10's *"some wholes correctly live nowhere"*, stated against C7's reading of extent as leaving the whole nowhere-in-particular and treating that as a **gap**, is the same shape as a whole with no hosts: **a whole that is the answer to *what is here* at no host's scale has an empty cover.** One question, answered once. **The fork it forces, stated and deliberately not chosen** --- inherited from [PR-9](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register), which declined it and routed it here. **Note the asymmetry, because it is what makes this sharper than an edge case:** PR-9's root rule (*the root is in every bucket*) holds **vacuously** over zero buckets, but totality (*every node in at least one bucket*) **fails for any non-empty graph**. Zero hosts is therefore a **guaranteed** totality violation, not a rare one. So either **(i)** zero-host wholes are unrepresentable, or **(ii)** totality is **conditional on the whole being hosted at all**. **Decided 2026-08-21 from conversation: (ii).** A hostless whole is **representable**, and totality reads **over hosted wholes** --- a whole with no hosts is **out of the invariant's domain** rather than in violation of it. **The shipped witness is what makes this the cheap answer:** `repairCharacterLegalPlacement` persists disconnected characters today, so (i) would have obliged either a migration or a standing lie about live data. **C10 pushes toward (ii)**, and (ii) qualifies PR-9's point (3), which currently reads *unconditional over the graph as it exists*. ~~**[PH0d](AGENT.presence.planning.md#recommended-order) grades this; do not close it from this row.**~~ **Overtaken 2026-08-21: closed from conversation to (ii), and PH0d closed without writing a case.** **What this row does not do:** choose a carrier (PR-3), name the walk (PR-4), or decide whether apprehension scale is world truth or perspective-relative --- **that stays PR-4's inherited question and the split does not touch it.** **One obligation opened, and it belongs to the parent rather than here; booked here because this row's split is what exposed it.** Nodes --- or `ludicGraph`s --- may need classifying on **whether they are apprehensible** (a grappling hook gun is, a computer network is not) and, **separately, whether they are manipulable**. **The two align in every case written so far** (gun yes and yes, moonbase computer no and no) **which is precisely why they should be booked as two rather than assumed to be one axis** --- no case yet separates them. **The list is open-ended and is not claimed to be these two.** **The carrier was worked the same day and the two halves separated. The edge-*instance* option is struck for both, and the test that struck it is recorded because it is reusable: *does this vary between two edges to the same parent?*** **Apprehensibility sits on the whole.** You do not apprehend the moonbase computer from a router and fail to from a terminal; what makes it *look* edge-borne is two wholes each answering for itself, which is C10's own *"from a terminal you see a terminal"* and needs no edge at all. **Ask that question of any future member of this list before giving it a carrier.** **What would revive the instance-level option, named so the strike stays falsifiable:** two parts of one contraption, both `PartOf`, one a battery that pops out and one soldered in. If the fiction wants that difference, manipulability **does** vary between two edges to the same parent. **No case tests it and it would be cheap to write** --- though the likelier reading is that what differs is the battery rather than the hosting, which puts the answer back on the part or its state and not on the edge. **Manipulability sits on the relation *kind*, and in doing so stops being a classification at all.** [LC8](AGENT.abstractionLayers.ludicCache.corpus.planning.md#lc8-the-spring-in-the-box-and-in-the-contraption)'s spring looked like edge-borne evidence --- `In` a box, `PartOf` a contraption's mechanism --- but nothing varies per **instance**: every `PartOf` into the contraption behaves alike, and so does every `In` into the box. **What the kind fixes is which mutations the edge admits.** `In` admits **rebinding** --- retarget the host in one move. **`PartOf` admits only make and break**, with an intermediate state in which the part is `In` something: the unbolted router is in the chassis, on the bench, or in your hands, **never nowhere**. **So there is no attribute to tag and no field to maintain --- the obligation is to state each hosting kind's mutation grammar**, which is smaller than the classification this paragraph was opened for. **Two things fall out.** **(i) Mild evidence on this row's own fork:** disassembly never yields a zero-host object, so the readiest candidate for *a whole that lives nowhere* turns out to be hosted after all --- which leaves [C10](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c10-the-moonbase-computer-system)'s scale case looking like the genuine article rather than one instance of a common pattern. **(ii) This is a discipline to add, not a description of shipped code.** [`MutationKernelTransferStep`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/manipulation/kernel/kernelStep.ts) is `{ entityIds, fromHostIds, toHostId }` --- **a rebind in one step** --- and relational edges are mutated by separate establish/dissolve steps, so **nothing today stops a single transfer moving a `PartOf`-hosted router straight into a spaceship.** The decomposed sequence is expressible but not required. **Deferred 2026-08-21, explicitly and narrowly: whether the constraint may be *composed away* at the action layer** --- one command *swap the lens* decomposing into three kernel steps, so that the rule never forbids good one-move fiction. **It defers cleanly on the user's own argument: Infocom-era games required the decomposed sequence and were perfectly playable story-games**, so nothing is at risk while it waits. **What does not defer with it is the primitive-layer grammar above.** **One argument is owed whenever the deferred half is taken up:** this reads as *adding a hard rule at the primitive layer*, which [PQ-12](AGENT.abstractionLayers.proposals.planning.md#open-questions-ab-34-sub-questions-ids-stable-never-reused) reversed away from --- the answer is presumably that it constrains a primitive's **shape** (make/break rather than rebind) instead of imposing a validity rule over a primitive that already exists, **but that has to be argued rather than assumed.** **Worked from conversation 2026-08-21; the halves above are findings, the deferred question is not a leaning.**

### Status cell, final state before graduation

**Answered 2026-08-21 --- three demands answered, the fourth split** (apprehension scale kept as truth, noticeability rehomed to the parent). **Not graduated**, though ~~the zero-host fork **(i) versus (ii)**, which [PH0d](AGENT.presence.planning.md#recommended-order) grades~~ **is no longer among the reasons --- resolved to (ii) on 2026-08-21.** **Opens a parent-side obligation, narrowed the same day from *classify nodes or graphs* to two smaller things:** apprehensibility declared on the **whole**, and each hosting kind's **mutation grammar** stated (`PartOf` admits make/break, not rebind) --- with the action-layer composition question deferred. **The zero-host fork gained a shipped witness 2026-08-21 from [PR-2](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register), and it is the first one that is not fiction:** [`repairCharacterLegalPlacement`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/membership/repairCharacterLegalPlacement.ts) reads `containers.length > 0` as *in play*, so **a disconnected character is a whole with zero hosts, represented and persisted today**. ## PR-2. Are character presence and whole extent one relation or two?

**Graduated 2026-08-21.** Verdict and date live in the [Settled register](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register); this is the derivation, moved verbatim from the row rather than rewritten. **The restriction this row settled is a scoping decision with a stated lift condition, so this record is the place to read *why it lifts* --- but the rule itself is [`AGENT.contract.md`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/AGENT.contract.md), written there 2026-08-21, and the enforcement question left as [PR-13](AGENT.presence.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only).**

**Row references:** Locked-frame clause 3; `AGENT.concepts.md`; PR-9, PR-10; ~~PH0c~~; [C2](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c2-the-rope-in-two-rooms-refused-at-the-gate), [C10](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c10-the-moonbase-computer-system); [AB-54](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#settled-register)

### Argument as the row carried it

**Are character presence and whole extent one relation or two?** See [the table above](AGENT.presence.planning.md#two-things-are-called-presence-and-whether-they-are-one-thing-is-pr-2). Locked-frame clause 3 argues one; single-valuedness, ~~the terminal `multiPresent` gate,~~ the separate shipped machinery and a mutation rate differing by orders of magnitude argue two. **The gate is struck 2026-08-21 --- it was a mis-citation and never argued anything about characters; see [the table above](AGENT.presence.planning.md#two-things-are-called-presence-and-whether-they-are-one-thing-is-pr-2), where it is corrected at source.** **A third answer is available and should not be reasoned past: one relation with a declared cardinality constraint on the character case.** **Work [PR-9](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register) and [PR-10](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register) first** --- if PR-10 holds, this row loses its representation half entirely (characters would carry no presence record to compare) and what remains is purely a question about the fiction. **Answered 2026-08-21 from conversation: the third answer, one relation --- but the constraint is on host *kind*, not on host *count*, and that difference is the whole content of the answer.** **The restriction: a character's membership host is a `ROOM`, and only a `ROOM`.** Objects are unrestricted and may be hosted by objects, features and areas as they already are. **Cardinality is not declared separately because it is a consequence:** a room is never a *member* of any graph --- the kernel's [`MutationKernelTransferStep`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/manipulation/kernel/kernelStep.ts) admits only objects and characters as `entityIds` --- so a room never inherits ports, so a character hosted in a room is single-**presence**-ported by construction. **This upgrades the premise [PR-10](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register) flagged as *a fact about the current tag graph, not a theorem*.** It is still not a theorem, but the residual premise moved from *no character's host happens to be partitioned* --- an accident of what has been authored --- to *rooms are never members*, which is enforced in a type. **Why the restriction, in the terms it was given, because the reason governs when it lifts: it is a scoping decision taken to keep the character path simple until there is a working graph system for non-characters.** **It is not a finding that characters are ontologically unlike objects, and must never be cited as one.** Read against [the scoped-boundary rule](../../../../AGENT.designVariant.md): the boundary was drawn against the immaturity of the non-character graph work, so what lifts it is that work maturing --- not new fiction, and not a corpus case. **The revisit is expected rather than hypothetical.** **Three of the four arguments for *two* dissolve, and the fourth changes job.** *Single-valuedness* is now port arity 1, the same property that makes a lantern's presence implicit --- a different count, not a different relation. *The `multiPresent` gate* was never a character gate: [`evaluateComplexityPreGates`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/actions/enrich/objectManipulation/complexityPreGates.ts) is typed on `EphemeraObjectId`, sits under `objectManipulation/`, and its only caller is `compileMembershipAtomic` --- **it gates objects, and is [C2](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c2-the-rope-in-two-rooms-refused-at-the-gate)'s refusal on the object side, so it argued sameness all along.** *Separate shipped machinery* is thinner than it looks: connect and disconnect compile through `buildCharacterMoveOp` -> `compilePositionKernelOp` into ordinary kernel steps, and what is genuinely character-specific is the eviction ladder and the narration, not the presence write. *Mutation rate* survives, but **not as an argument about the fiction** --- it is a cost, and it lands on the option this row declined; see the torture-device case below. **The answer is already implemented and was not recognised as an answer.** [`resolveCharacterRoomId`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/membership/resolveCharacterRoomId.ts) narrows containers with `isEphemeraRoomId` before reading them, and `syncMembershipAdjacency` does the same. **That filter is this restriction, shipped and undocumented** --- the same shape as PR-10 finding the existential invariant was its own answer already in code. **Consequence worth stating plainly: hosting a character in an object's graph is *type-legal today* and would not fail loudly.** An Object is a legal `EphemeraMembershipHostId` and the kernel already admits a character as a transferable entity, so the transfer would succeed --- and then the character would have zero *room* containers, fall through to the `RoomStack`, and read as **out of play**. **A restriction enforced by a read-side filter fails silently by construction**, so if this row's restriction is to be relied on it needs a stated rule rather than four independent narrowings; **booked, not written here.** **The case that decided it, and it is better than the one this row was going to be given: a character strapped to a torture device, or standing on a table.** The choice is between a **peer** kind (`Custom`, with a `relationLabel`, character stays a room-level node) and a **hosting** kind (`On`, character becomes a member of the object's graph) --- which is [AB-54](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only)'s own partition, so the case tests exactly one variable. **Answered: `Custom`.** **And the reason inverts what this plan had assumed about `Custom`.** Its opacity --- [the validator requires a non-empty author-supplied `relationLabel`](../../../../../packages/mtw-interfaces/ts/ephemeraMeta.ts) that no generic rule can read --- was first written up here as its *cost*. **It is the mechanism.** An opaque peer edge is what routes *what happens if someone lifts the table* to the reasoning engine; a hosting edge pre-answers it structurally, and answers it silently. **The engine firing on that question is the design, so the edge that provokes it is the right edge.** **What this therefore rules out for now, named as a feature rather than as a gap: structural immobilization.** A hosting edge would make *leave the room* inexpressible without first transferring the character out --- [PR-1](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register)'s mutation grammar applied to a character, and a real thing to want. **It is deferred with the restriction and returns with it.** Until then a restraint is a peer edge plus whatever the reasoning engine makes of it, and **nothing in the presence graph enforces it.** **The cost of the option declined, recorded so the revisit does not have to rediscover it:** under hosting, a strapped character sits in the device's bucket, so *who is in this room* stops being one adjacency query and becomes an aggregation walk --- on the highest-frequency read in the system. **That is where the mutation-rate argument actually bites.** Note it does **not** make the character un-apprehensible: apprehensibility sits on the whole ([PR-1](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register)), and a person strapped to a device is an answer at room scale in a way [C10](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c10-the-moonbase-computer-system)'s router is not. **The cost is in the read path, not in the fiction.** **One reclassification this row makes on the way past: `RoomStack` is not presence.** [`resolveCharacterRoomId`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/membership/resolveCharacterRoomId.ts) consults it **only** when membership is empty, and what it returns is the top frame after trimming to accessible assets --- *where should this character be placed on re-entry*, not *where is it*. **It is a different relation sharing a read path, and unifying presence does not touch it.** **Falls out to other rows.** **[PR-3](AGENT.presence.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only) loses characters entirely** for as long as the restriction holds, since a room-hosted character is single-ported by construction and PR-3 is the multi-ported carrier question. **[PR-1](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register)'s zero-host fork gains a shipped witness**, since [`repairCharacterLegalPlacement`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/membership/repairCharacterLegalPlacement.ts) reads `containers.length > 0` as *in play* --- a disconnected character **is** a whole with zero hosts, represented and persisted; it grades toward fork (ii), ~~**with the honest caveat that fork (i) may answer that a disconnected character is *out of the world* rather than *in the world at nowhere*, and that distinction is exactly what (i) would owe.**~~ **Caveat discharged 2026-08-21: (ii) was taken, so this witness is now confirming evidence rather than a lean.** A disconnected character is **in the world at nowhere** --- representable, and simply outside totality's domain. **[AB-54](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only) gains a second amendment** alongside the one already booked for PR-4's partitioning class: character-to-object relations take **peer** kinds, so AB-54's partition is now constrained on the character side and should say so. **And a terminology hazard, flagged because it will be conflated: `kind: 'On'` is a *hosting* kind, while a character on a table is `kind: 'Custom'` with `relationLabel: 'On'`.** The same word on two fields means opposite things about where the character lives. **Prefer a different label in examples, or state the distinction wherever `On` appears.** **What this row does not do:** it does not merge the shipped code. [`syncMembershipAdjacency.ts`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/membership/syncMembershipAdjacency.ts) and [`syncObjectMembershipAdjacency.ts`](../../../../../lambda/ephemera/dataSource/positions/membership/syncObjectMembershipAdjacency.ts) are identical line-for-line apart from the identifier name, which is now justified as duplication rather than as coincidence --- **but the dedup has a shelf life**, because both filter to rooms and only the character one is *supposed* to. **Whether the object one filtering to rooms is already wrong for a boxed object is a question this row raises and does not answer**; it has not traced that function's callers. **Design-only, no code in scope.**

## PR-9. Is the cover total, and what does it range over?

**Graduated 2026-08-21.** Verdict and date live in the [Settled register](AGENT.presence.planning.md#settled-register); this is the derivation, moved verbatim from the row rather than re-summarised.

**Row references:** Locked-frame clause 1; PR-4; CC1b; PH0d

### Argument as the row carried it

**Requirement: is the cover total --- must every part be somewhere?** Stated in the [cover formulation](AGENT.presence.planning.md#presence-as-a-cover): is there any circumstance in which we would want a node to be a member of a whole's graph and yet **fall in no bucket** --- present in none of the hosts the whole is present in? **The lean is that presence is obligatory**, and the cover is total: a part in no bucket makes *where is it* undecidable for something the model has agreed to hold. **One sub-question is definitional and must be settled in this row rather than deferred: what does the cover range over --- nodes only, or nodes and edges?** An ordinary (non-`Present`) edge whose endpoints fall in different buckets --- the rope segment crossing the doorway --- **is covered by nothing said so far**, and it is either in both buckets, in neither, or the boundary is itself a node. A cover over nodes and a cover over nodes-and-edges are different objects, and the reducer will care which. **This is the parent's [CC1b flag](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#recommended-order) --- node presence and edge composition being the same operation --- arriving from the presence side.** **Answered provisionally 2026-08-21 from conversation, on three points.** **(1) The cover is total, and it ranges over *nodes only*.** **(2) The edge half dissolves rather than being decided, and shipped design is what dissolves it.** [CC1b](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#recommended-order) joins a boundary-spanning edge out of two --- the room's `boulder -TiedTo-> ROPE:4` and the rope's interior `rope-end -TiedTo-> (egress)` --- into one edge carrying `crossings`. **Read in the presence direction, which is the reverse of CC1b's, a boundary-spanning edge is already two edges terminating at ports, one lying wholly inside each bucket.** So the doorway edge is not an edge in no bucket; it is not one edge. ~~**The definitional sub-question is therefore answered by not arising**, and the `nodes`/`edges` cover distinction never has to be drawn.~~ **RE-OPENED the same day by [PR-C1](AGENT.presence.corpus.planning.md#pr-c1-the-contraption-in-two-rooms-and-whether-covers-nest), and the closure above was too broad rather than wrong.** **CC1b's argument is about *graph* boundaries** --- one host's shard and another's --- **and PR-C1 produces an edge that crosses no graph boundary at all**: a cable whose two spans are both nodes in `GrapplingHookGun`'s own graph and whose endpoints sit in different **buckets** of it. **Bucket boundaries and graph boundaries are not the same thing, and point (2) assumed they were.**
