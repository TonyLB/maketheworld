## Proposal EP-1: the Arrangement --- an edge-side abstraction as a component with outbound membership

### What EP-1 is for: warrants, not permission

**This proposal exists because rows were carrying requirements whose reason lived nowhere citable.** [EA-8](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#settled-register) answers *do edges need identity?* with **yes, optionally** --- and that yes is not argued on general grounds and not drawn from the corpus. It is warranted by what the Arrangement needs. With no proposal to name, the row anchored itself to [EA-7](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#ea-7)**(b)**, a candidate that EA-7's own grading **closed**; the live warrant survived only as prose inside EA-8's Status. **A warrant pointing at a rejected candidate is the failure mode the parent's *check the named destination* rule exists to catch**, and it went unnoticed because nothing was structurally wrong --- the citation resolved, the row read as complete.

**Amended 2026-08-28 by EA-8's graduation, and the first two sentences above no longer hold.** That *yes* **is** argued on general grounds and **is** drawn from the corpus: it graduated on the principle that **structural keying is a uniqueness constraint on the fiction rather than a neutral reference mechanism**, grounded on [EA-C6](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.corpus.planning.md#ea-c6-the-thread-that-jams-in-the-keyhole), with EP-1's clause 3 demoted to **confirmatory rather than load-bearing** --- deliberately, because a row held up by one proposal falls with it. **This does not retire the paragraph, it relocates what it is evidence of.** EP-1 is still the reason the requirement was *written down*; it is no longer the reason the requirement *holds*. **The failure it records is unchanged and is the durable part**, since it was never a fact about edge identity.

**So the sentence EP-1 makes writable is:** *this is required not on general grounds and not from a corpus case, but because the Arrangement requires it, and the Arrangement is being carried forward.* Rows may cite EP-1 for that. **What EP-1 does not do is license building** --- a warrant explains why a requirement is held, and the tier system decides what may be built.

### Status

**Provisional, and not cheap.** EP-1 consolidates design already settled or answered in rows, plus the against-code assessment no row has done; it changes no row's disposition. **Clause 1 is four union admissions and a stored record**, which is foundational implementation work rather than a widening --- priced in *What it costs*.

**Its evidence is inherited, and discounted.** Clause 2 is [EA-6](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#ea-6)'s answer (a candidate, untested against a corpus case) and the reification frame is [EA-7](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#ea-7)'s resolution to (a) (resolved on argument, not per-case graded). **EP-1 is no better evidenced than the rows it consolidates**, and writing it up does not improve their standing.

**Clause numbers are stable and cited** --- [EA-8](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#settled-register)'s warrant names clause 3. **This record's headings are title-bearing**, by the sibling convention of [the parent's proposal record](AGENT.abstractionLayers.proposals.planning.md) and unlike the ID-anchored `### EA-N` sections next door, **so retitling a proposal here costs an inbound-citation sweep** and costs nothing there.

### What EP-1 extends: the shipped `ludicGraph` vocabulary

Stated once, from [`ephemeraMeta.ts`](../../../../../packages/mtw-interfaces/ts/ephemeraMeta.ts) as verified 2026-08-28, because every clause below is written against it. **Re-verify against the file before relying on any bullet here.**

- **`EphemeraLudicGraphData`** is `{ hostId, rootId, nodes, edges?, ports }`. The stored field payload omits `hostId`, and is carried as an optional **`ludicGraph`** on Object (MK2), Feature (MK3) and Area (MK4) meta --- **not on Character or Room**.
- **`EphemeraLudicGraphNode`** is a **closed tag union of five kinds**: Character, Object, Room, Feature, Area. Each carries a `universalKey` of the matching `Ephemera*Id`. The union's own comment records that widening it *"is not a part-of-ladder claim"* --- a tag says a kind may appear in a graph's node list, not that it sits above the room in the containment ladder.
- **`EphemeraLudicTerminalId`** is `EphemeraLudicTerminalPrimitive | EphemeraLudicPortAddress`, the latter `{ owner, port }` --- and **`owner` is typed `EphemeraLudicTerminalPrimitive`, not "a node."** That primitive union is **the same five kinds** as the node union, and the identity is deliberate: LP4b widened the node union precisely so that no terminal could *"name no backing node."*
- **Ports and graph-ownership are typed against a fourth union, `EphemeraMembershipHostId`** --- `EphemeraLudicGraphData.hostId`, `EphemeraCrossingPort.fromHostId` and `EphemeraPresencePort.fromHostId` all take it. **Nothing about holding a port is typed against `EphemeraLudicGraphNode` at all.** Its comment carries the same not-a-part-of-ladder guard, and records Area as **host-only** --- a host that is never itself a member, and so has no counterpart on `EphemeraPositionAdjacencyContainedId`.
- **The four unions are therefore a stack, and admission to the top of it buys nothing on its own.** A kind present only in `EphemeraLudicGraphNode` can be named by no edge, can own no port, and can host no graph. **LP4b's referential-integrity gap pointed the other way round** --- a terminal naming no node --- **so it does not cover this direction, and both must be checked.**
- **`EphemeraLudicRelationalEdgeData`** is `{ tag, from, to, kind }`, plus `relationLabel` when `kind` is `'Custom'`. **It is a value with no id**, matched structurally. Nodes have ids; ports carry `portId`; **edges have nothing.**
- The validator **checks `from` and `to` independently**. Any variant admitted to `EphemeraLudicTerminalId` is therefore legal on *either* side at once.

### The proposal

1. **An Arrangement is a component type**, sibling to Object, Room, Feature and Area. It takes an `EphemeraArrangementId` in `EphemeraId`; enters `EphemeraLudicTerminalPrimitive`, so edges and port addresses can name it; enters `EphemeraLudicGraphNode`, so it can appear in a node list; and enters `EphemeraMembershipHostId`, so it can hold ports and own a graph. It stays out of `EphemeraPositionAdjacencyContainedId` --- **host-only, as Area is.** **The four admissions are a stack and the top is not sufficient alone**: hosting and ports are typed against terminals and hosts, never against `EphemeraLudicGraphNode`, so a node-only Arrangement could be named by no edge and could hold no port. **This is EA-7(a)'s reification argument** --- one vocabulary, no second hosting story --- **carried to the depth the types require.**
2. **Its member edges remain its siblings.** They stay in the graph they already occupy, terminating on the nodes they actually relate. **Nothing is contained and nothing moves.**
3. **Membership is an outbound reference list held on the Arrangement**, using the optional edge identity of clause 4. `PartOf` is **dispensed with entirely** for this construct: members are siblings, not parts.
4. **Edge identity is minted optionally, per edge** --- for the edges something needs to name; **most will not have one.** **This clause spans two rows, and they are not the same claim.** That an edge may *carry* a label is [EA-8](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#settled-register), and it is all clause 3's membership list needs. That the label may *stand where a terminal stands* --- the primitive is single and shared, `EphemeraLudicTerminalId` gains an edge-reference variant, and because `from` and `to` validate independently **port-to-edge and edge-to-port arrive together**, so restricting which is used is a **design rule, not a type constraint** --- is [EA-10](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#ea-10)'s, and only the port legs need it. **Clause 3 therefore does not wait on the second half.**
5. **Extent is derived, never stored.** An Arrangement's host height is the **join over its member edges' terminal positions** --- the rule [C6](AGENT.abstractionLayers.corpus.planning.md#c6-the-rope-the-candle-and-the-impromptu-timer) already establishes for node-wholes. It **moves when a member edge's terminal moves.**
6. **An Arrangement licenses; it never derives.** It is evidence that a node abstraction may be worth minting, and a node abstraction is equally evidence that an Arrangement may be --- **the relation runs both ways and neither direction forces anything.** This is [AB-26](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only)'s shape one scale up but **one notch weaker than AB-26's own sense**: grounds to consider, not authorization to record. Do not read the shared word at equal strength.
7. **Recognition is not player-visible.** It adds a node and some references and touches nothing else; member edges are untouched. **Presence therefore scopes rather than depicts** --- an Arrangement is present to bound which rooms it is a candidate in, not to appear in an inventory.
8. **An Arrangement does not wrap its part-role edges in an interior, though it may use an interior for other purposes.** The wording is deliberate: *part-role*, not *member*, because [whole and part are roles](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#what-is-inherited-as-settled-and-must-not-be-re-argued-here) and an edge holds that role only relative to the pairing being asked about.
9. **An Arrangement is authorable, and only its WML syntax is deferred.** Component-hood in this codebase *means* authored --- every `EphemeraId` tag but `ASSET` has a `standardize/components/` file --- so clause 1 commits to it either way, and **the commitment is made deliberately rather than acquired by side effect: Arrangements will eventually be written and saved in WML.** What is deferred is the **tag shape**, and the reason is that syntax is the expensive thing to be wrong about --- **the construct is still in flux, and fixing a syntax now buys technical debt rather than authoring.** The prototype proves out in Ephemera first. **Clause 7's *recognition* is therefore not exclusive**: an Arrangement may be recognized from beneath or declared from above, and nothing here makes recognition the only origin. This resolves an axis the rows left open --- **EA-6 and EA-7 never said whether an Arrangement could be declared at all** --- and it is settled by decision, not by evidence.

### What it buys (assessed against code, 2026-08-28)

- **No second machinery.** Reifying to a node means **no second hosting story, no second port story, and no second conflict rule.** Hosting is already recursive, and *whole* adds nothing to *host*. This is the cost EA-7(b) --- a containment graph between edges --- would have incurred and the reference form never does.
- **The port-leg shape invents no new *form* of terminal, but it needs the whole of clause 1.** Legs of the form `Thread#portN -> Arrangement#portA` are ordinary port addresses, a shape the vocabulary already has --- so clause 1's four admissions plus clause 4's identity variant are the entire change. **They are not free**: `EphemeraLudicPortAddress.owner` is `EphemeraLudicTerminalPrimitive` and both port branches carry `fromHostId: EphemeraMembershipHostId`, so these legs typecheck only once Arrangement is in **both** of those unions.
- **Completeness becomes a set comparison against data the entry already carries, never a stored flag that can be set wrong.** The Arrangement node holds the reference list, so *"is this assembly complete?"* is answered from membership. **This matters outside this plan:** [LC4](AGENT.abstractionLayers.ludicCache.corpus.planning.md#lc4-the-string-that-is-only-half-opened) records that nothing validates a completeness marker's honesty on the node side and that a referential-integrity check is therefore owed; **on the Arrangement side there is no marker to be dishonest.** [LC9](AGENT.abstractionLayers.ludicCache.corpus.planning.md#lc9-the-place-setting-laid-across-two-tables) is contingent on this.
- **It is uniform where the withdrawn containment form split.** Containment worked for a coarse edge authored top-down over *new* interior detail and broke for an Arrangement recognized bottom-up over edges that already existed, because containing those would move them. Clause 2 holds **by construction rather than by argument** in both directions.
- **It answers PQ-1's bar in the scoping direction.** Presence scopes the Arrangement so recognition need not scan every Arrangement globally. **This is not a discharge of [PQ-1](AGENT.abstractionLayers.proposals.planning.md#the-port-record-split-complementary-with-the-interior-authoritative-settled-2026-08-06)'s *what does it save* bar** for the construct as a whole --- see what it does not resolve.

### What it costs

- **Clause 1 is four union admissions and a stored record, and this is the foundational work.** An `EphemeraArrangementId`; entry in `EphemeraId`, `EphemeraLudicTerminalPrimitive`, `EphemeraLudicGraphNode` and `EphemeraMembershipHostId`, with the four typeguards each of those carries; a `Meta::Arrangement` row; and a `ludicGraph` field on it as the next in the MK2/MK3/MK4 series. **Area is the template** --- the most recent kind to walk this exact path, host-only in the same way, and readable in the tree rather than imagined. **LP4b's referential-integrity property must be checked across the new tag in both directions**: no terminal naming a kind with no backing node, and **no node kind that no terminal can name.** *Where an Arrangement is written down* is not an open question under this clause --- a component type has an id series and a `Meta::` row by construction --- **it is a line item.**
- **An Ephemera-first component has no precedent here; the reverse has three.** Guidance, Mark and Lens are WML components with no `EphemeraId`. **Nothing runs the other way** --- every `EphemeraId` tag but `ASSET` has a `standardize/components/` file. `EphemeraId`'s own comment says the allowlist is *"maintained by hand,"* so nothing forbids the ordering clause 9 chooses, **but Arrangement would be the first, and a first is worth knowing you are taking.** The cost is carried in the gap and is proportional to its length: for as long as it lasts, an Arrangement can exist in play with **no authored source to round-trip back to**, so nothing in the WML layer can be relied on to reconstruct one.
- **Optional identity produces a mixed population**, and this is the sharpest running cost. Some edges are matched structurally and some by id, so **every lookup site must handle both.** [EA-8](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#settled-register) made this a named precondition rather than a caution: *enumerating the code that relies on structural matching is booked nowhere, blocks building, and nothing here should be implemented before it is done.* **Discharged 2026-08-28 and struck as a running cost:** the enumeration was done and **all sixteen non-test sites can carry a mixed population**, so this is no longer a cost EP-1 pays --- and the field shipped with EA-8's graduation. **What replaced it is smaller and sharper.** `edgesMatch` is **blind to the id by design**, so structure is still the only authority on edge sameness; the mixed population is safe precisely *because* nothing reads the id yet. **The cost EP-1 still owes arrives when a constructor mints one** --- at that moment the id-versus-structure question is forced, and a test pins it so it cannot be answered by accident.
- **Identity inherits identity's questions, and the shipped code has already answered one of them by construction.** *Whether two structurally identical edges in one graph are one edge or two* is settled at **one**: `addRelationalEdge` returns unchanged when a structural match is stored, so the edge set is deduplicated on `(from, to, kind, relationLabel)` and **a second identical edge is unrepresentable rather than ambiguous.** Minting identity **lifts that constraint**, which is a change to what the fiction can contain and not merely an addition to how it is addressed. **What remains open is continuity**: `HostRelationalPatch.op` is `'add' | 'remove'` with no update, so changing an endpoint or a label is delete-plus-add and yields a different edge by construction --- **clause 3's membership list would silently drop the member across such a change.** See [EA-8](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#settled-register)'s verdict, which names the update op as one of the three things an id does **not** deliver; the disjunct-(ii) argument behind it is in [the derivation](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.discussion.planning.md#ea-8-do-edges-need-identity). **Unchanged by that graduation** --- nothing has proposed an update op, and this clause is still exposed.
- **Derived extent is load-bearing rather than optional.** The containment form gave extent for free; clause 5 has to compute it, and it changes when a member edge's terminal moves. **Nothing currently recomputes it.**

### What it changes conceptually

- **Edge abstraction is purely additive; node abstraction relocates.** Recognizing an Arrangement moves nothing: fine edges stay where they are and the render is unchanged. A minted *node* abstraction takes its parts into its shard, which **is** a render change. **The asymmetry is principled and will not need patching --- nodes have position and edges do not**, so node-abstraction necessarily changes where things are while edge-abstraction has no *where* to change.
- **An edge cannot be relocated without being replaced**, because an edge **is** its endpoints. This is the argument that withdrew the containment form, and it is [EA-5](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#ea-5)'s asymmetry one level down.
- **Staleness is one-directional, not two-way reconciliation.** A coarse edge can be falsified by a change beneath it, but it holds **complementary** facts and cannot *disagree* with the interior about facts it does not hold. That is PQ-1's ingress/egress shape --- **precedented, not novel.**
- **An Arrangement justifies zero or more coarse edges.** The definite article in *the* whole-scale edge presupposed a one-to-one tree that [EA-1](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#settled-register) retired.

### What it does not resolve

- **What decides that a mint is worth making.** Not answered here; it is [AB-64](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only)'s edge-side recognition question.
- **Whether minting an Arrangement produces the host-level mirror [AB-57](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only) is looking for.** That is [AB-58](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only)'s second clause and **stays the parent's question**; EP-1 supplies the construct it asks about and takes no position on the mirror.
- **PQ-1's *what does it save* bar, for the construct as a whole**, and [AB-62](AGENT.abstractionLayers.planning.md#open-decisions-design--plan-only)'s materialization size. Both are owed and neither is paid.
- **Whether the definition itself is gradeable by fiction.** [EA-6](AGENT.edgeAbstractions.planning.md#ea-6) flags that it may not be, in which case the honest form is a stipulation with a stated re-open trigger rather than a corpus hunt. **EP-1 does not decide that**, but it does change what the question is asking: a proposal is graded on *what it buys, assessed against code* and *what it costs*, which is a bar this file can meet and a decision row could not.
- **A discriminating corpus case.** EA-7 resolved on argument and records *"no corpus case has been run against these"*; EA-6's answer is a candidate untested against a case. **EP-1 adds no evidence to either.**
